Step by step

“I won’t have time to wrap everything nicely in December.”

Yes, you will. Five steps – and the trickiest part is the bow.

The steps are the same for all five looks. See them with Pink & gold or Gold stars:

  1. Fold the bottom of the postal box and prepare the lid.

    Tip: Start by folding along all score lines before assembling. It makes the process easier.

    See the pink postal box→

  2. First, place the ribbon at the bottom of the postal box so the ends stick out over the sides. Fold the tissue paper to fit the width of the postal box and lay it in the bottom of the box on top of the ribbon.

    Tip: Make sure the ribbon goes well over the edges so there’s enough for a bow – it makes step 5 easier.

    See the tissue paper→

  3. Place them in the centre of the paper, with the logo facing up – it's your business card inside the parcel.

    Tip: Are the boxes loose? Crumple up an extra sheet next to them so nothing rattles in transit.

    Boston ECO – free logo print→

  4. Place a fir sprig and a candy cane on top – or a wooden star from us.

    Tip: Decorations can come from the garden or kitchen drawer – just make sure they look intentionally placed.

    See the wooden stars→

  5. Take the ends of the ribbon up over the paper, tie a bow, and close the lid. Now it’s not just an order – it’s a Christmas gift.

    Tip: Tie the bow inside the postal box – not around it. That way, the lid can close over the bow.

    See the green satin ribbon→

All the practical details

Seven answers before you order.

Which postal box should I choose?

Start with what you’re shipping: choose a postal box with room for your products and the paper around them. The internal measurements are shown on each product page.

Kraft, pink, and black close without tape. The white one has a tape closure. All five are natural brown inside, so it’s the paper that adds the colour.

View all postal boxes→
How many should I buy – and where should I store them?

Without a logo, you order postal boxes in packs of 50. They’re delivered flat and unassembled, so they take up minimal space in storage – and you fold them by hand without tools.

Can I have my logo on the postal box?

Yes – foil on the outside of the lid, digital print on the inside, or both. This costs extra per box, and with logo you order from 200 pieces. If you want your logo in gold, silver or white, choose foil – digital print cannot do that.

Production takes 5–6 working days when you reorder with the same logo, and 8–9 working days with a new logo.

Is logo print on jewellery boxes really free?

Yes – all five jewellery boxes in the looks include free logo print: Boston ECO, Melbourne ECO, Santorini ECO, Seoul ECO and Vienna. On your first order you pay neither setup nor cliché charges.

Would you like a Christmas design on the lid? Choose a ready-made Christmas motif in the logo module.

See the Christmas motifs→
Can I get tissue paper and ribbon with logo?

Yes, but not for this Christmas. With a new logo, production time is 6–7 weeks, and tissue paper with logo is ordered from 5,000 sheets.

This year, pack with in-stock paper and ribbon – and next Christmas, your logo can be on everything.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes – of postal boxes and jewellery boxes, also with your logo. Samples are not free: you pay for shipping and a small fee per sample with logo.

When should I order for Christmas?

If you want a logo, the answer is: now. Without logo, processing time is 1 working day. Deadlines differ from country to country, so check your own.
