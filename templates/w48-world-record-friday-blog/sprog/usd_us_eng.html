Black Friday

“So you still aren't doing Black Friday?”

No, we went for world records instead. It might have been a bit too ambitious…

Four colleagues each made a different world record attempt, and of course we filmed everything. None of the official world records were broken, but we got five videos and a bit more respect for the people who do this professionally. See for yourself how it went.

The presenter summed it up pretty well: “There were questionable techniques, definitely.”
