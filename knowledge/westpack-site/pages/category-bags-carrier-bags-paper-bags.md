Choose the right paper carrier bags for your products
Size and shape matter as much as finish. Small bags suit ring boxes, earring boxes , gift cards, vouchers and compact cosmetics. Medium formats are often a better fit for jewellery boxes, candles, folded knitwear, skincare sets and standard gift items. Larger paper carrier bags are more suitable for boxed gift sets, folded apparel and multi-item purchases, while bottle bags are designed with a narrow base and taller shape for wine, spirits, oils or similar products.
Check width, height and gusset together rather than looking at one measurement in isolation. The gusset affects how well a bag holds boxed products and bulkier purchases, while the height determines whether tissue paper, ribbon closures or taller packaging can sit neatly inside. For jewellery and gifting, it is usually best to match the bag to the outer box dimensions so the pack looks balanced at the till rather than oversized or too tight.
Paper bags with handles: twisted, flat and rope handle options
Paper bags with handles are the main buying route for most retail businesses, but the handle style changes both look and use. Flat handle bags are a practical choice for higher-volume counters and straightforward transactions. Twisted handle bags offer a more familiar retail carrier format with a slightly smarter finish while still working well for everyday use. Rope handle bags sit at the more premium end and are often chosen for boutiques, gift packaging and higher-value purchases.
Construction details help buyers compare value and presentation properly. Stronger paper weights, reinforced tops and a more structured base usually matter more for heavier boxes, candles, cosmetics and gift sets than for lighter purchases. If the bag is part of the presentation, a rope handle or boutique gift bag format often justifies the upgrade. If the priority is cost control across large volumes, twisted or flat handle paper carrier bags are usually the better fit.
Brown, kraft, white and black paper bags
Colour and material choice can change the feel of the whole handover. Brown paper bags and kraft paper bags are often chosen for natural, understated retail packaging, while white paper bags give a clean base for tissue paper, ribbon, stickers or logo print. Black paper bags and darker finishes are usually better suited to premium boutiques, jewellery shops and gift packaging where the bag should feel more elevated.

Paper bags FAQs
Below are answers to common trade questions about selecting, ordering and branding paper bags for retail use.
What size paper bag do I need for jewellery boxes or small gifts?
For ring boxes, earring boxes, gift cards and other compact items, a small paper bag is usually the right choice. For larger jewellery boxes, candles, cosmetics or small gift sets, a medium format is often more practical. Check the bag width, height and gusset against the outer box size so the product sits neatly inside and still leaves room for tissue paper if needed.
What is the difference between twisted handle, flat handle and rope handle paper bags?
Flat handle bags are typically chosen for practical, high-volume retail use. Twisted handle paper bags offer a familiar carrier style that balances cost and presentation for everyday shop sales. Rope handle paper bags are usually used for more premium retail presentation, especially in boutiques, jewellery shops and gift packaging where the bag is part of the purchase experience.
Can I order paper bags wholesale for my shop or chain of stores?
Yes. Westpack supplies paper bags wholesale for business buyers, including independents, multi-site retailers and chains. Stocked lines are a good fit for repeat orders, visible pack pricing and quicker dispatch, while larger-volume planning can help support seasonal demand, promotions and store rollouts.
Are printed paper bags and custom paper bags available with my logo?
Yes. If you need branded paper carrier bags, printed paper bags or paper bags with logo, custom options are available. Plain stock bags and branded bags usually follow different ordering routes, with custom orders more likely to involve minimum order quantities, artwork approval and a longer lead time than stocked products.
Which paper bags are suitable for bottles, candles, cosmetics or gift sets?
Bottle bags are the best option for wine, spirits and other tall narrow products. Candles, cosmetics and boxed gift sets usually need a bag with enough width and gusset to hold the product securely without forcing the sides. For heavier or more premium items, it is worth considering stronger paper weights, reinforced tops and higher-end handle styles.
