Payments can be made by bank transfer, selected credit cards, SEPA direct debit (EUR only), etc.
Credit card payments
Credit card payments are made online, using our SSL payment system (Secured Socket Layer).
When prepaying an order online, by credit card, the full amount of the order is reserved on the customer’s account by WP. The payment will not be debited until the order has been invoiced.
WP can never debit more than the amount that was reserved when the payment was processed.
Due to this, any changes made in an order that has been (pre)paid online, that result in a modification of the total amount, may require for a new or additional payment to be made.
When changes are made to an already (partly) prepaid order, the order can not be processed until the payment has been received in full, regardless of any payment credit the customer might have.
When the final order is for less than the reserved amount, the payment will be debited effective immediate, when the definitive order confirmation has been sent.
All credit- and debit card holders are subject to validation and authorisation by both the issuer of the card and WP, for security reasons and the prevention of fraud. Financial details are treated confidentially, and will not be passed on to any third party, nor stored for later use.
