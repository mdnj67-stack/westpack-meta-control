Bags for jewellery and gift packaging are designed for business buyers who need practical packaging for retail presentation, gifting and dispatch. Our range includes retail bags, packaging bags and bags for jewellery packaging in formats suited to rings, earrings, bracelets, necklaces, watches and small gift items.
Choose from paper bags , luxury gift bags, carrier bags, jewellery pouches and bags and organza bags, with options for different sizes, colours, finishes, handles and closures. Whether you need plain stock for fast ordering or custom printed bags with your logo for repeat store use, you can buy in trade-friendly pack quantities and build a coordinated packaging range with jewellery boxes, tissue paper, ribbon, stickers, gift wrap and pouches.
Choose your bag type
Use this Bags category as a starting point. Choose jewellery pouches and bags for small jewellery items, carrier bags for retail handover, gift bags for premium presentation, or repair bags for service and workshop use.
Bags for business buying
Choose bags by product type
Small bag formats help you match packaging to what you actually sell, from ring boxes and earring cards to bracelet boxes, necklace boxes , watches and small gift items. This makes it easier to avoid oversized packaging and keep presentation consistent across different price points.
Order wholesale bags in practical pack sizes
Our bags are suited to trade ordering, whether you need smaller pack quantities for seasonal lines or bulk volumes for daily store use. Stock bags are available for quick ordering, while repeat ordering is simple when you want the same format, colour or print setup across multiple locations or ongoing campaigns.
Add branding when presentation matters
Custom printed bags give jewellers, boutiques and gift retailers a clear way to carry branding beyond the sale. Logo print options are useful for in-store purchases, event packaging and online orders where branded presentation helps create a more complete customer experience.
Wholesale bags for retail packaging
If you are buying for a shop, studio, counter or ecommerce, wholesale bags need to work on both presentation and day-to-day handling. Our assortment covers practical carrier bags for regular sales, luxury gift bags for more elevated purchases, and smaller packaging bags for jewellery and gift items that need a neater fit.
Trade buyers can choose formats that suit different selling environments, including in-store purchases, event packaging and e-commerce presentation. Look for pack quantities that match your sales volume, stock visibility on plain bags for faster replenishment, and product cues such as bestseller, custom print available, premium finish and fast delivery when comparing options.
Bags for jewellery packaging and small gifts
For jewellers and gift retailers, size and format matter more than broad category labels. Small bags are often used alongside ring boxes, earring boxes , bracelet boxes, necklace boxes and watch boxes, giving you a finished handover without moving into oversized retail packaging. Fabric pouches and organza bags can also work well for smaller items where a softer presentation is preferred.
When choosing bags for jewellery packaging, start with the outer dimensions of the boxed or wrapped item rather than the jewellery piece alone. A close fit helps the bag look tidy, supports the product in transit from store to customer, and avoids wasting tissue paper or ribbon when building a gift-ready presentation.
Choose the right packaging bags by size, handle and finish
Different packaging bags suit different uses. Paper bags and carrier bags are a practical choice for everyday retail packaging. Luxury gift bags are better suited to premium presentation and gifting. For smaller items, pouches and organza bags offer compact formats that can sit inside larger branded packaging or be used on their own for selected product lines.
Check bag dimensions against your product width, depth and height, especially if you are packing boxed jewellery or adding tissue paper , ribbon or stickers. Handle styles may vary by bag type, including twisted paper handles, ribbon handles, die-cut handles or no handles on smaller gift bag and pouch formats. Finishes and colours also affect the final look, from simple everyday options to more polished surfaces for higher-value purchases.
Custom printed bags for retailers and repeat orders
Custom printed bags are a strong fit for retailers who want consistent branding across stores, events or online shop packaging. Adding your logo can help tie together bags, jewellery boxes, tissue paper, stickers and ribbon so the full packaging set feels considered rather than pieced together from separate suppliers.
Plain stock bags are usually the quickest option when you need packaging fast. Printed bags take longer because artwork, approval and production need to be scheduled, so they are best planned for repeat ordering and ongoing use. If you are deciding between plain and printed, think about lead time, order volume and whether the bag will be used as everyday packaging or as part of a more premium branded presentation.
Bags for shops, boutiques, events and ecommerce orders
Different selling channels call for different bag choices. Shops and boutiques often need a mix of everyday retail bags for standard transactions and luxury gift bags for special purchases. Event sellers may want compact branded bags that are easy to carry and quick to pack at busy stands. Webshops often use bags as part of the unboxing presentation inside a dispatch parcel rather than as the outer shipping pack.
If you want a joined-up packaging range, pair your bags with jewellery boxes, tissue paper, ribbon, stickers, gift wrap and pouches. This makes it easier to build a clear presentation level for each product category, from simple everyday packing to more premium gift packaging, while keeping ordering more consistent across your packaging supplies.

Bags FAQs
Here are answers to common questions from business buyers choosing bags for jewellery packaging, gift presentation and retail use.
Can I order bags with my logo printed on them?
Yes. We offer custom printed bags with logo options for retailers, jewellers, boutiques and webshops that want branded packaging. Printed bags are a good choice when you want a consistent look across your bags and related packaging such as boxes, tissue paper, ribbon or stickers.
Which bags are best for jewellery packaging?
That depends on how the item is packed and presented. Small paper bags, luxury gift bags, fabric pouches and organza bags are all used for jewellery packaging. If the jewellery is in a box, choose the bag by the box dimensions. For rings, earrings, bracelets, necklaces and watches, a close-fitting bag usually gives the cleanest result.
Do you offer wholesale bags in bulk pack sizes?
Yes. Our bags are available in trade-friendly pack quantities suited to business demand, from regular store use to larger replenishment orders. This makes it easier to buy for everyday retail packaging, seasonal peaks and repeat purchasing across ongoing product lines.
What is the lead time for printed bags compared with plain stock bags?
Plain stock bags are usually the faster option because they are ready to order from existing stock. Custom printed bags take longer as they require artwork approval and production time. If you have a launch date, event or seasonal campaign, it is best to allow extra time for printed orders.
Can I order matching packaging such as boxes, tissue paper and ribbon?
Yes. Bags can be combined with jewellery boxes, tissue paper, ribbon, stickers, gift wrap and pouches to create a coordinated packaging range. This is useful if you want your in-store, gift and webshop presentation to follow the same visual style across different product types.
