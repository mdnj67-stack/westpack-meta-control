Gift card boxes for retail presentation
Gift card boxes work well when the presentation matters as much as the card itself. Retailers use them to upgrade in-store gift card sales, while spas, salons and gift shops often use gift voucher boxes to turn a simple printed voucher into a product that feels ready to hand over at the till. For jewellery businesses, card gift boxes are also useful when a voucher is sold with a small add-on item such as earrings, a charm or a bracelet, especially when paired with jewellery boxes or gift bags .
If the item is customer-facing, presentation should still be practical. Look at the internal dimensions first, then check the box depth and closure type so the contents sit neatly without moving around. Colour and finish can then be chosen as part of your retail presentation, whether you need something understated for everyday use or a more branded look for premium collections and store credit cards.
Choose the right gift card box size and closure
The fastest way to choose gift card boxes is to start with format. Credit card-sized boxes are best for standard gift cards, loyalty cards, membership cards and similar card formats. DL or voucher-sized boxes are better when the contents are longer, folded or printed on a larger stock size, such as spa vouchers, event tickets, campaign cards or repair collection cards used by jewellers.
Closure style also changes the fit and the feel. Rigid two-piece boxes are a good choice for straightforward retail presentation and efficient packing. Where available, magnetic gift card boxes are better when you want a more structured closing action for higher-value presentation, corporate gifting or branded promotional use. You can also explore magnetic gift boxes for larger presentation formats. If you are comparing options, check whether the internal dimensions suit standard gift card sizes and whether the box depth gives enough room for an insert or an extra item.
Branded gift card boxes or plain stock gift card boxes
Plain stock boxes are the practical option when you need packaging quickly, want to test a new format or need flexibility across different stores or campaigns. They are often the easiest route for short-term promotions, seasonal voucher launches or businesses that need stock ready for dispatch without waiting for print production.
Branded gift card boxes make more sense when presentation needs to stay consistent across multiple sites, repeat campaigns or retail collections. Logo printed boxes are commonly used by gift shops, jewellers, spas, salons and corporate brands that want a more recognisable handover experience. In most cases, stock items are available on shorter lead times, while custom printed orders require longer planning but give you stronger brand consistency for repeat use.

FAQs about gift card boxes
Here are some of the most common questions from trade buyers choosing gift card boxes, gift voucher boxes and card gift boxes for retail or promotional use.
What size gift card boxes fit standard gift cards?
Credit card-sized gift card boxes are usually the right choice for standard gift cards, loyalty cards and membership cards. Always check the internal dimensions rather than the outer size, and compare that with the exact card measurements, insert type and required box depth.
Do you offer card gift boxes in both card-sized and DL voucher formats?
Yes. The range includes card-sized formats for standard gift cards and longer DL or voucher-sized boxes for printed vouchers, tickets, repair collection cards and other promotional or retail cards that need more length.
What is the difference between a magnetic gift card box and a rigid two-piece box?
A rigid two-piece box has a separate lid and base, which makes it a practical option for everyday retail packing. A magnetic gift card box has a folding lid with a magnetic closure, which suits branded presentation, corporate gifting and higher-value voucher packs where the closing action is part of the experience.
Can gift voucher boxes be printed with a logo?
Yes. Branded and logo printed gift voucher boxes are available for businesses that want a more consistent retail or promotional presentation. Plain stock boxes are typically the faster option, while printed orders usually come with a longer lead time depending on quantity, format and print specification.
Do you offer wholesale gift card boxes or bulk order pricing?
We support wholesale gift card boxes and bulk gift card boxes for trade buyers ordering for stores, chains, campaigns or repeat promotional use. Pack quantities and reorder options help businesses buy in volume and keep packaging consistent across repeat orders.
