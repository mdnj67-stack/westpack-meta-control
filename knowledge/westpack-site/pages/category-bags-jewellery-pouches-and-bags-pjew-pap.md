Choose our acid-free paper bags for safe and secure packaging
Acid is a common culprit when it comes to silver jewellery tarnishing, which is why our jewellery envelopes are designed to be acid-free. Unlike traditional packaging materials, which can contain harmful acids that accelerate tarnishing, our paper bags provide a safe and secure packaging solution for your precious jewellery items.
When silver comes into contact with acid, it can cause the metal to corrode and turn dark, diminishing the piece's beauty. By choosing our acid-free paper bags, you can be confident that your jewellery is safe and protected from harm.
