If you ship products to your customers regularly, the right packaging accessories make packing faster, safer and more consistent. Alongside postal packaging , you may need void fill, tissue paper, protective wrap, tape, packing tools and other dispatch materials to help orders arrive in good condition.
When customers order from your online store, they expect the same level of care when the parcel arrives as they received during the buying process. The right packaging accessories help protect jewellery boxes, gift boxes, pouches, vouchers and small accessories from movement, pressure and transit damage.
Packaging accessories for safer ecommerce dispatch
-
Void fill
If you need void fill for packaging, choose a material that fills empty space without putting too much pressure on the product. Curled kraft paper, tissue paper , kraft tissue paper or kraft paper can help keep the packed item more stable inside the box.
When using postal boxes , it is important to choose the right size so you are not shipping unnecessary empty space. A closer fit helps reduce movement, filler use and packing time.
-
Corrugated paper
Corrugated paper can be wrapped around larger or more exposed items that need extra protection before being placed in an outer box. It is useful when you need a flexible protective layer that can be cut to the right size for the item being packed.
-
Bubble wrap
Bubble wrap is easy to use when preparing delicate items for shipment. Wrap the item, place it in a suitably sized box, and fill any remaining space so the product cannot move around during transit. For lighter orders that do not need a rigid box, mailing bags, postal bags and padded envelopes may be a better fit.
Tools and finishing products for packing orders
Useful packing tools include scissors, a hobby knife, tape, tape gun, packing list sleeves and labels. These small items make a big difference when you are packing repeat ecommerce orders or preparing larger batches for dispatch.
If you are sending flat jewellery orders, vouchers, cards or slim gift sets, you may also want to compare standard packaging accessories with letterbox jewellery boxes . These are useful when you need a more structured presentation than an envelope, but a slimmer format than a standard postal box.
Once the practical packing materials are in place, you can focus on the customer experience. Pair protective packaging with tissue paper, stickers, pouches or inserts to create a neater unboxing experience for jewellery, gifts and accessories.
