Clear acrylic displays are suitable for jewellery in all kinds of materials. The glossy transparent displays do not take focus from your beautiful jewellery. Instead, they allow them to be fully in focus.
Our product range contains individual pieces, e.g.for displaying rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, but also blocks in various sizes. Blocks are popular to create height and improve the visibility of products in your window display and on retail counters.
Many of the displays can also be printed with a logo, at a small surcharge.
