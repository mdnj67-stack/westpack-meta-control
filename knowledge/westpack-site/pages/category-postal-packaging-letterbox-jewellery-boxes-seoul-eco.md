Advantages of Seoul ECO Jewellery Boxes
The Seoul ECO jewellery box is produced from a more robust material compared to standard options. Its impressive 1.5 mm thick cardboard provides a superior luxury experience. This enhanced durability makes it an excellent choice for shipping, ensuring your pieces are well-protected during transport. Furthermore, its low profile flat drawer design is not only practical but also aesthetically appealing to your clientele.
Customisation and Professional Presentation
At Westpack , professional presentation is a priority. The Seoul ECO design offers a sleek, minimalist look that can be easily customised with your brand’s logo, enhancing your brand recognition and positioning. Our complimentary logo print and expert design assistance ensure your jewellery box series will reflect the high quality of the jewellery it holds.
Series Features and Functional Design
The Seoul ECO jewellery boxes feature a flat drawer design that is user-friendly, allowing easy opening and closing. Its robust build and high-quality materials make it a top choice for sustainability-minded businesses. The design is optimised for e-commerce, fitting easily through standard letterboxes, which can significantly reduce postage costs while maintaining a professional image.

FAQ

What makes the Seoul ECO jewellery box suitable for shipping?
The Seoul ECO jewellery box is crafted from thicker materials, specifically 1.5 mm cardboard, providing robust protection ideal for shipping. Its compact, flat design ensures it can be easily slipped through letterboxes, reducing shipping costs.
Can I customise my Seoul ECO jewellery boxes?
Yes, Westpack offers free logo printing on all jewellery boxes, including the Seoul ECO series. Our design experts work with you to ensure your logo appears flawless, adding a personal touch that enhances your brand's image.
