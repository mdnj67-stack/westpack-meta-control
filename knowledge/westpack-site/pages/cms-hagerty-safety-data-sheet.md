Safety Data Sheet
Safety data sheet for jewellery polish (MSDS)
When using and storing cleaning products, the labour inspection demands for you to have workplace instructions.
Therefore, you need a Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS).
The relevant MSDSs can be found below - just click the data sheet you need and accept the dialog.
In order to view the documents you will need Adobe PDF Reader, which you can download via the link https://get.adobe.com/reader/
It is only necessary to download Safety Data Sheets for the products found on the premises.
