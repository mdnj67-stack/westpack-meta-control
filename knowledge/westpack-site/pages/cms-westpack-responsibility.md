UN'S SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS
OUR COMPANY IS BASED ON FOUR VALUES:
RESPONSIBILITY, TRUSTWORTHINESS, COMMUNITY, AND BUSINESS ACUMEN.
Therefore, CSR is in our DNA. However, to help ourselves and our suppliers meet actual, measurable goals and standards, it is also part of our strategy, our practices, and our policies. To ensure that we adhere to the fundamental principles of responsible business conduct, Westpack has made the strategic decision to support the UN Global Compact initiative. By being part of this global and well-recognised initiative, we believe we have the best possible framework for integrating strategies, policies, and procedures that will secure Westpack’s long-term success.

OUR COMPANY MAKES A DIFFERENCE
WESTPACK IN ASIA
Operating an ethically responsible business in Asia requires a level of diligence and caution that is not as necessary in Europe, where most of our customers are based. Staying true to Westpack’s core values, we go to great lengths to ensure a sustainable impact in the countries where we operate.
Westpack is a full member of amfori, which provides access to the BSCI platform. This platform is renowned for its transparency in the monitoring process. Another key advantage is amfori Academy, which offers training and e-learning for members. This will allow Westpack’s employees in Asia to expand their expertise and stay up to date on CSR matters. Employees at our suppliers’ facilities will also have access to amfori Academy, further strengthening Westpack’s value chain.
Although we are transitioning to a new audit platform, we will continue our close collaboration with Bureau Veritas, which will conduct BSCI audits for us. Bureau Veritas has been assisting us in auditing factories since 2014.
WORKING ENVIRONMENT AND OPPORTUNITIES
At Westpack, we believe that a good working environment embraces differences and fosters innovation. Our employees’ well-being is both a moral responsibility and a crucial necessity for running a successful business. We could not achieve what we do without our employees thriving. Many of our team members have been with Westpack for more than 10, 20, or even 40 years, and we take great pride in that.
Westpack has an annual profit-sharing scheme, where all employees receive a fixed percentage of the profits. Everyone—from machine operators to key account managers, from interns to accountants—receives the same amount. This has been our practice since 1989. The strategic reason behind our annual profit-sharing is to foster a sense of community in the workplace. It is also a way to show that every single employee makes a difference.

SUPPLIERS AND SUBCONTRACTORS
Westpack's mission is to ensure that all our internal affairs align with both the UN Global Compact and the Sustainable Development Goals. Equally important is our commitment to maintaining responsible CSR practices throughout our supply chains. The entire organisation adheres to the principles of the UN Global Compact and the 17 Sustainable Development Goals. This applies across the entire value chain that Westpack is responsible for and has the ability to influence.
Westpack's Code of Conduct serves not only as a guideline for the business conduct expected of our suppliers but also as a tool for continuously assessing them. Suppliers must grant Westpack and/or any representatives acting on behalf of Westpack unrestricted access to production facilities, employees, and all relevant data necessary for auditing in accordance with SA8000 principles. Audits may be conducted with or without prior notice.
Westpack Code of Conduct
Westpack's Code of Conduct is rooted in the values on which Westpack was built. We are committed to these values, which include trustworthiness, community, and, most importantly, responsibility. These values align with the standards of SA8000. Here are some key aspects of our Code of Conduct:
- The supplier must not employ individuals under the age of 15.
- The supplier must have an employee representation policy.
- Employees of the supplier must not be discriminated against based on race, colour, nationality, religion, gender, age, sexual orientation, or marital status.
- Corrupt agreements with suppliers, officials, or other third parties are strictly prohibited, meaning that Westpack enforces a zero-tolerance policy.
- Working hours must comply with applicable legislation. If no such legislation exists, Westpack sets a maximum working day of 8 hours per day and 48 hours per week.
