Favour boxes are a practical way to present small gifts, confectionery and branded event items for weddings, parties, hospitality, retail and corporate gifting. At Westpack, this range focuses on empty favour boxes only, with trade-friendly options including pillow boxes, cube boxes, truffle boxes, clear favour boxes and selected favour bags for lightweight items.
Whether you need wedding favour boxes for venue styling, party favour boxes for event packaging, or presentation boxes from our wider gift boxes & bags selection for boutique retail and business gifting, you can choose by size, shape, closure, colour and finish. White favour boxes remain one of the most requested options, and selected lines are suitable for logo printing or branded packaging to support personalised favour boxes for business use.
Trade-friendly favour packaging for repeat business use
Choose formats that fit the product
Use cube and truffle styles for chocolates and sweets, pillow boxes for jewellery and miniature gifts, and clear favour boxes where product visibility matters. This helps buyers match packaging to earrings, charms, gift cards, soaps, candles and small promotional items without guesswork.
Order in packs that work for events and resale
Our favour boxes UK range is suited to both scheduled event work and day-to-day retail packing, with pack sizes and bulk quantities that support wedding planners, confectioners, boutiques and other trade buyers. Stock visibility and quick dispatch help keep repeat orders simple.
Add branding without changing the product category
Selected personalised favour boxes are available through logo printing or branded packaging options, giving business buyers a clean way to promote their brand on wedding favours, hospitality gifts and corporate event packaging. You can also finish plain boxes with ribbon, tags, wholesale tissue paper or inserts.
Shop favour boxes by style
Different box styles suit different products and packing workflows. Pillow boxes are useful for slim items such as bracelets, earrings, vouchers and miniature gifts. Cube boxes work well for balanced presentation and are often chosen for candles, soaps and wrapped treats. Truffle boxes are a strong option for confectionery and premium chocolate presentation, while clear favour boxes help when the product itself needs to be visible on display trays , display tables or retail counters.
You can also browse selected favour bags where a softer format is more suitable, especially for lightweight items or quick packing at scale. Keeping these formats clearly separated makes it easier for trade buyers to choose packaging that suits the item, the occasion and the packing time available.
Wedding favour boxes and party favour boxes for trade buyers
This range is built for business use rather than one-off party supply shopping. Wedding favour boxes are widely used by wedding stylists, venues, florists and confectioners for table favours, guest gifts and coordinated event presentation. Party favour boxes are also suitable for branded launches, hospitality events, seasonal promotions and corporate gifting where a small but polished pack format is needed.
If you are buying for ongoing event work, wedding favour boxes wholesale options and volume-friendly pack quantities can make planning easier. Many buyers return to the same box style across multiple events to keep presentation consistent, simplify ordering and reduce packing time for staff.
Personalised favour boxes for branded presentation
For business buyers, personalised favour boxes are less about individual messages and more about brand recognition. Selected products can be used for logo printing or branded packaging, making them suitable for boutiques, jewellers, hotels, bakeries and companies sending event gifts or promotional packs. This is especially useful when the product inside is small but the presentation still needs to look considered.
Personalised wedding favour boxes with a company logo or venue branding can support premium event work without moving into consumer-style custom gifting. If you need a plain stock option, white favour boxes give a clean base for ribbon, labels, tags or printed sleeves.
Choose the right favour boxes by size, closure and finish
When selecting favour boxes, start with the product dimensions and how it will be packed. Jewellery and gift cards usually suit shallow or slim formats, though dedicated ring boxes or earring boxes may be better for more specific presentation. Chocolates, soaps and candles often need a more structured cube or truffle box. Check the internal size carefully so the item fits neatly without excess movement, especially for premium presentation or transport to venues.
It is also worth considering closure type and assembly format. Flat-packed favour boxes save space in storage and are practical for larger event runs, while more rigid assembled options can speed up packing where presentation time is tight. Colour and finish matter too. White favour boxes are consistently popular for weddings and boutique gifting, while clear lids or windows can help show product detail for confectionery and retail display.
Bulk ordering and wholesale favour boxes for UK businesses
For buyers managing repeat events, seasonal gifting or retail packaging, reliable supply matters as much as box style. Our favour boxes UK selection is designed to support trade ordering with practical pack sizes, stock-backed lines and quick dispatch on standard products. This helps businesses plan ahead for weddings, hospitality work, promotional activity and regular resale packaging.
To complete the pack, pair favour boxes with ribbon, tissue paper, wrapping paper for event gifts , inserts, tags, gift bags or carrier bags depending on the item and the finish you need. These related packaging accessories can help protect the product, improve presentation and create a more consistent branded look across your range.

Frequently Asked Questions About Favour Boxes
Here are some of the most common questions from trade buyers looking for favour packaging for events, resale and branded gifting.
What are favour boxes used for in a business setting?
Favour boxes are used as empty presentation packaging for small items such as jewellery, chocolates, sweets, soaps, candles, gift cards and miniature gifts. They are commonly bought by wedding professionals, confectioners, florists, boutiques, jewellers, hospitality venues and corporate gifting teams that need a compact, presentable pack format.
Do you offer personalised favour boxes with logo printing?
Yes, selected lines can be used for personalised favour boxes through logo printing or other branded packaging options. This is aimed at business branding rather than personal messages, making it suitable for retail presentation, event packaging and corporate gifting.
Can I order wedding favour boxes wholesale or in bulk quantities?
Yes. Many trade buyers order wedding favour boxes wholesale for event runs, venue work and repeat seasonal demand. Volume-friendly pack sizes make it easier to buy for larger quantities while keeping presentation consistent across multiple jobs or locations.
Are favour boxes supplied flat-packed?
Many favour boxes are supplied flat-packed for easier storage and efficient transport, though the format depends on the product style. Flat-packed options are useful for larger event orders, while some structured designs may offer faster packing once assembled. Always check the product details for the exact assembly format.
Which favour box size is best for jewellery, chocolates or gift cards?
The right size depends on the item dimensions and the presentation you want. Pillow boxes are often chosen for jewellery and gift cards, truffle boxes for chocolates and sweets, and cube boxes for soaps, candles and small gifts. If product visibility is important, clear favour boxes can be a good option for wrapped confectionery or decorative items.
