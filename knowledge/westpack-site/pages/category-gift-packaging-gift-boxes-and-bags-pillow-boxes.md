Why choose pillow box packaging for business use?
Easy to store and quick to assemble
Flat-packed pillow boxes take up less shelf and stockroom space than assembled gift boxes , which helps if you hold multiple sizes or colours. They are quick to fold into shape, making them useful for retail counters, order packing and seasonal peaks.
Low-profile presentation for lightweight items
For jewellery, gift cards and compact accessories, pillow boxes give a neat finished look without adding unnecessary bulk. They work well for shelf display, inside gift bags and for lightweight ecommerce orders where presentation still matters.
Clear stock and repeat-order support
Trade buyers need dependable formats they can reorder with confidence. Westpack makes it easier to buy in the quantities you need, check stock availability, see ex VAT pricing and choose between standard stock lines or custom pillow boxes for brand consistency across stores or events.
Wholesale pillow boxes for retail, gifting and ecommerce
For shops and ecommerce teams, pillow boxes offer a simple way to package lightweight products neatly and consistently across retail and online channels. They are a strong fit for jewellery counters, gift shops, boutiques, subscription packs, event favours and ecommerce orders where you want a compact box that looks finished without slowing down packing operations.
If you are comparing formats, pillow box packaging often sits between jewellery pouches and bags and rigid gift boxes. It gives more structure than a pouch, a cleaner display shape for shelves and baskets, and a lower unit cost and smaller storage footprint than many rigid options. For businesses ordering at volume, bulk pillow boxes can also help standardise presentation across multiple locations or repeat campaigns.
Choose the right pillow box size for jewellery and small gifts
Exact dimensions matter when you are packing jewellery and compact gift items, so each product listing should be checked against the item you are packing. As a practical guide, small pillow boxes are best for earrings, charms and delicate jewellery where a close fit helps avoid movement. Medium sizes are often used for bracelets, pendants, gift cards and small gifts that need a little more room without looking oversized.
Larger pillow boxes can work well for wider accessories, small gift sets, scarves or grouped items, provided the product depth and width are matched properly to the box dimensions shown. Using the size guide this way helps avoid under-filled packs, reduces wasted void space and makes packaging easier for lightweight orders with postal boxes .
Stock pillow boxes or printed pillow boxes?
Stock pillow boxes are usually the fastest route if you need packaging now, want lower entry quantities or are trialling a new format. They are available in straightforward trade-friendly options that make repeat ordering simple, with clear quantity choices and ex VAT pricing to support budget planning.
Printed pillow boxes are a better fit when brand visibility is a priority, especially for jewellery retailers, boutiques, event gifting businesses or resellers that want a consistent branded presentation. If you need logo branding, custom pillow boxes give you more control over colour, finish and presentation, while stock lines remain the practical choice for short lead times and quick dispatch.

FAQ: Ordering pillow boxes for business use
Here are some of the most common questions from business buyers ordering pillow boxes for jewellery, gifting and ecommerce use.
What products are pillow boxes best suited for?
Pillow boxes are best suited to lightweight products such as earrings, pendants, bracelets, charms, gift cards, wedding favours, small accessories and other compact gift items. They are popular with jewellery retailers, gift shops, boutiques, online sellers and event gifting businesses that need a neat presentation format without the bulk of rigid boxes.
Which pillow box size should I choose for earrings, bracelets, pendants or gift cards?
Use the exact dimensions shown on each product page and match them to the item you are packing. As a general guide, small pillow boxes suit earrings, charms and delicate jewellery, medium sizes suit bracelets, pendants, gift cards and small gifts, and larger sizes can be used for wider accessories, scarves or compact gift sets.
Are pillow boxes supplied flat-packed and are they easy to assemble?
Yes. Pillow boxes are supplied flat-packed, which helps save storage space and makes them practical for stockrooms, shops and fulfilment areas. They are quick to fold into shape, so they work well for busy retail counters, order packing and event preparation.
Can I order plain stock pillow boxes in bulk or choose printed pillow boxes?
Yes. You can buy plain stock options for fast ordering and day-to-day use, or choose printed pillow boxes if you need logo branding and a more tailored look. Bulk pillow boxes are useful for trade buyers who want better unit costs, consistent presentation and straightforward repeat ordering.
What are the lead times, pricing and reorder options for business buyers?
Stock items are generally the best option for quick dispatch, while custom pillow boxes and printed orders usually involve longer lead times. Product pages should show quantity options, stock visibility and pricing ex VAT where applicable, making it easier to plan trade orders and reorder the same style again for consistent store presentation.
