Exquisite Craftsmanship and Design
The Sofia jewellery box stands out with its elegant velour surface, offering a luxurious finish that speaks volumes about your brand's commitment to quality. Its hinged lid allows for a classic and exclusive reveal, enhancing the unboxing experience for your clients. Every Sofia box comes with a customisable foam insert tailored to secure your jewellery pieces perfectly, providing both protection and presentation. Learn more about our diverse jewellery boxes that cater to different styles and preferences.
Customisation and Branding Opportunities
What sets Westpack apart is our ability to enhance your brand through personalised solutions. With the Sofia series, you benefit from free logo printing on the interior, ensuring that your brand is prominently displayed with a polished touch. Our expert in-house designers collaborate closely with clients to ensure your logo is reproduced perfectly, reinforcing your brand identity with each unveiling. Explore our full range in different jewellery box series for a variety of materials and finishes.
High-Quality Materials and Easy Logistics
Westpack’s Sofia jewellery box is made with materials that provide a premium feel with robust protection, essential for maintaining the quality of fine jewellery during storage and transport. The design is not only visually appealing but also practical—crafted to fit efficiently into retail environments and to optimise shipping solutions for e-commerce businesses. The compact design ensures ease of handling and cost-effective logistics.
Trusted Supplier Since 1953
