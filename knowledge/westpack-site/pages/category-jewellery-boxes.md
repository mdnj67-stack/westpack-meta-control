Choose the right jewellery packaging boxes by size, insert and closure
The best jewellery packaging boxes depend on product dimensions, display needs and how the item will be sold or dispatched. Small items such as rings and studs usually suit compact boxes with a secure slot insert, while necklaces and chains often need longer formats that keep the piece positioned neatly. Bracelet, bangle and watch styles usually require more internal depth or a wider insert shape to avoid movement in transit and on display.
Insert types can include foam, wadding, velvet-look or multi-use inserts depending on the box style and intended use. Closure styles also affect presentation and packing efficiency, with options such as lift-off lid, hinged lid, magnetic closure and drawer style formats. For retail counters, a fast-opening box may suit everyday handling; for gifting and premium collections, a more structured presentation box may be the better fit; for postal orders, stable internal fit and efficient outer packing are key.
For customers who need jewellery storage beyond the main presentation box, you can also offer travel jewellery cases as a practical add-on for rings, earrings, necklaces and bracelets.

Frequently asked questions about jewellery boxes
Below are answers to common trade questions about ordering, branding, sizing and delivery for jewellery boxes.
Can I order wholesale jewellery boxes in bulk quantities?
Yes. Westpack supplies wholesale jewellery boxes for trade buyers, with pack quantity options across different box styles and materials. You can start with smaller quantities for testing or sampling and then move to bulk jewellery boxes orders for repeat retail use, gifting programmes or larger stock requirements.
Can I add my logo to jewellery boxes?
Yes, bespoke jewellery boxes with logo branding options are available. Custom projects may include logo print, colour selection, material choice, insert options and finish details, depending on the box range and order volume. If you need branded packaging, it is best to review the required specification and lead time before placing a larger order.
Which jewellery box size is best for rings, earrings, necklaces or bracelets?
The right size depends on the jewellery type, dimensions and insert layout. Rings and studs usually fit compact boxes, earrings often suit small square or rectangular formats, necklaces need longer boxes, and bracelets or bangles typically need wider or deeper formats. Always check available box sizes and dimensions together with the insert type to make sure the item sits securely and presents correctly.
Are inserts included with the jewellery boxes?
Many jewellery boxes are supplied with inserts, but the insert type varies by product. Common options include foam, wadding, velvet-look or multi-use inserts. Product details should always be checked to confirm whether the insert is included and whether the format is designed for rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, watches or jewellery sets.
Can I order samples before placing a larger order, and how quickly can jewellery boxes be delivered?
Yes. Sample ordering is available on many lines so you can check size, colour, insert fit and overall presentation before committing to a larger purchase. Delivery times depend on stock availability and whether you are ordering standard products or bespoke jewellery boxes, which usually require a longer lead time. If you are planning a launch or larger replenishment, trade order support can help you schedule the order more accurately.
