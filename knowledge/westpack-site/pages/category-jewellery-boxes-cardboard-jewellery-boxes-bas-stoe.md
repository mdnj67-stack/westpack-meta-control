Jewellery boxes with drawer pull-out
The Stockholm ECO jewellery box has a modern matchbox design . You pull out a neat little drawer from a cover with the help of a satin ribbon. In the drawer, you place your jewellery in the foam insert, which has all the classic cuts and a velour surface, ensuring your jewellery is beautifully presented .
The design of the jewellery box differs from other cardboard jewellery boxes , as classic boxes typically have a separate lid and base. By choosing this series; Stockholm ECO , you stand out from the crowd.
The jewellery box is made of FSC® certified cardboard with a light texture.
Personal branding
You can of course have your personal logo printed on the Stockholm ECO jewellery boxes, and it is included in the price. It looks absolutely stunning when the boxes are printed with, for example, copper, white, black, silver, gold... Yes, there are many options. Choose the print colour that best suits your branding and achieve a beautiful and personal look. A personal branding that your customers will recognise from purchase to purchase!
Are you unsure which print colour to choose for your logo? Order a sample first and decide afterwards. This way, you can be sure to achieve exactly the look you want.
