Could we grow together?
Become a Westpack Partner
Does your business use Westpack packaging as an active part of your brand experience? And do you love sharing your unboxing moments, daily work or workshop processes on social media?
Then you may be exactly who we are looking for. We are seeking passionate businesses and creative people for a close partnership where we help each other grow.
What we can create together
Mutual value and visibility
1. Partnership advertising (Meta Partnership Ads)
You create the visual, organic content (e.g. a video of your packing routine featuring our packaging). We fund and optimise the advert, which runs directly from your own profile. This gives your brand strong, paid visibility to a completely new audience – fully paid for by us.
2. Your story in the spotlight (customer cases)
We would love to tell the story of your brand. We invite you to be featured in a dedicated article on our website, which we also share in our newsletter and on our own channels. Here, you can showcase your workshop, inspire others and share your favourite setups.
What do we expect from you?
We value quality and good chemistry. For a partnership to work, we believe in a professional structure and mutual respect. We are looking for partners who:
- Care about aesthetics: You focus on strong image and video quality, as well as a consistent visual expression.
- Meet deadlines: You take pride in keeping agreements, so the collaboration runs smoothly and easily.
- Think long term: You see the long-term value in building a strong, close collaboration.
What’s in it for you?
A good partnership should always work both ways. As an official Westpack Partner, you and your business can look forward to:
- Strong brand awareness: We use our advertising budget and platforms to share your brand and your content with thousands of people.
- Exclusive partner benefits: We will, of course, make sure you are rewarded with benefits that support your business in the long run.
