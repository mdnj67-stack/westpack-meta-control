Branded tissue paper for trade orders
Logo printing included at no extra charge
We print your logo free on branded tissue paper , making it easier to carry your identity through to the inside of the pack.
Choose a sheet format with a consistent paper weight
Choose the format that suits your packaging while keeping the same tissue weight across your assortment. For a coordinated presentation, pair it with branded satin ribbon .
Review your logo before production
We email a digital proof within 1–3 working days. After you approve it, we send a sample photo for approval before production.
Choose a sheet size that fits your packaging
Choose between 350 × 500 mm and 700 × 500 mm sheets. Each product comes in one format, and the paper weight is 17 gsm throughout the range.
Match the sheet to the item and the space available inside its box or bag. Allow for the folds you use when deciding which format will work for your packaging.
Match tissue and print colours to your brand
Tissue colours include black, grey and white; logo-print colours include gold, silver, black and white. For custom tissue paper with your logo, consider the tissue and print shades together.
Colour combinations vary by product, and special Pantone print colours are available on request for defined brand colours. If you prefer a repeating design, compare it with tissue paper with pattern .
Plan folds around the one-sided logo print
We print the logo on one side of the tissue, and it may be faintly visible on the reverse.
Consider which panel will face the customer and whether the reverse will be exposed in your planned fold or layered wrap.
Plan order quantities and production time
The minimum order is 5,000 sheets. Estimated production time is 6–7 weeks for a new logo and 5–6 weeks for a repeat order.
These are production estimates, not delivery times. Keep production planning separate from delivery planning when scheduling a launch or replenishment.
Acid-free tissue for silver and jewellery
Our acid-free tissue does not tarnish silver, plated metals or jewellery. It is a relevant specification when tissue will wrap or sit against these products.
For mixed retail assortments, consider where this property matters most in your packing process, particularly for jewellery orders alongside general gift lines.

Branded tissue paper FAQs
Find answers to common trade-order questions about quantities, logo approval and the specifications that affect packaging decisions.
What is the minimum order for branded tissue paper?
The minimum order is 5,000 sheets. Factor that quantity into your packaging plan for the product range and your expected replenishment cycle.
What happens after I submit my logo?
We email a digital logo proof within 1–3 working days. Once you approve the proof, we send a sample photo for approval before production, giving you two review stages.
Which tissue and logo-print colours are available?
Tissue colours include black, grey and white, while logo-print colours include gold, silver, black and white. Combinations vary by product; special Pantone print colours are available on request.
How long does production take, and are these delivery times?
Estimated production time is 6–7 weeks for a new logo and 5–6 weeks for a repeat order. These figures cover production, not delivery, so allow separately for delivery when planning a launch or restock.
Is the tissue suitable for jewellery, and is the logo printed on both sides?
Our acid-free tissue does not tarnish silver, plated metals or jewellery. The logo is printed on one side and may be faintly visible on the reverse, so consider how the sheet will be folded around your products.
