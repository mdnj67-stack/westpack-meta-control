Frequently asked questions about gift boxes
Below are answers to common trade questions about selecting, ordering and branding gift boxes for business use.
Which gift boxes are best for rings, earrings, necklaces and bracelets?
Use item-specific jewellery gift boxes wherever possible. Ring boxes hold single rings securely, earring boxes are sized for studs or drops, necklace boxes give more length for chains and pendants, and bracelet or bangle boxes provide the width needed to avoid crowding. Inserts and pads are useful for keeping delicate items in place and improving presentation.
What is the difference between rigid, magnetic and flat-pack gift boxes?
Rigid gift boxes arrive formed and give a solid retail presentation. Magnetic boxes are often used for premium gifting and branded presentations where the opening experience matters. Flat-pack gift boxes save storage space before use and are practical for businesses holding larger packaging stocks or multiple sizes.
How do I choose the right size gift box for my product?
Start with the product dimensions and compare them to the internal box size, not just the external measurement. Allow enough room for inserts, tissue or ribbon if needed, but avoid excess space that lets the item move in transit. For jewellery and smaller accessories, a closer fit usually gives a better result in both presentation and protection.
Do you offer wholesale gift boxes and bulk quantities for businesses?
Yes. The range is aimed at business buyers and includes wholesale gift boxes and bulk ordering options across multiple box styles, sizes and finishes. This makes it easier to buy for stores, ecommerce fulfilment, events and repeat packaging runs with stock visibility and dispatch support to help plan orders.
Can I order empty gift boxes with logo printing and matching accessories?
Yes, selected empty gift boxes are available with logo printing for branded packaging. You can also build a coordinated packaging set with matching gift bags, tissue paper, ribbon, stickers, insert pads and mailing cartons, which is useful for retail presentation, launches and corporate gifting.
