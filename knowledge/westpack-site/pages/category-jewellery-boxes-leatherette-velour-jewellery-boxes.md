Choose velvet jewellery boxes by jewellery type and size
Selecting the correct box starts with the jewellery item. Ring, double ring and long ring boxes are designed for single bands, paired rings or elongated styles. Earring and stud boxes suit smaller pieces that need a stable insert position, while pendant and chain boxes provide a better layout for drops and fine necklaces. Bracelet and bangle boxes and watch boxes offer wider or deeper interiors, and necklace, half set and full set boxes are better suited to coordinated presentation.
When comparing options, check both dimensions and insert layout. Internal fit matters for stone height, band width, chain length and whether items should sit flat or upright. For businesses selling across multiple categories, coordinated ranges help maintain consistent presentation while still selecting the correct box size and format for each product.

FAQs about velvet jewellery boxes
Find quick answers to common trade questions about fit, sizing, range selection and ordering.
What jewellery items fit in each type of velvet jewellery box?
The range covers boxes for ring, double ring, long ring, earring, stud, pendant, bracelet, chain, bangle, watch, necklace, half set and full set presentation. The best choice depends on the insert layout and internal dimensions, so buyers should match the box to the specific jewellery item rather than selecting by exterior shape alone.
How do I choose the right size velvet jewellery box?
Start with the jewellery type, then check the box dimensions and insert style. Consider band width, stone height, chain length, bracelet depth or whether a set needs room for multiple items. For retail display, also think about how the box will sit in cabinets, on counters or inside outer gift packaging.
What is the difference between velvet, velour and leatherette jewellery boxes?
Velvet jewellery boxes are typically chosen for a soft, premium presentation. In many trade packaging ranges, this finish is achieved using velour materials, which provide a similar appearance with better durability for retail use. Leatherette jewellery boxes offer a smoother and more structured outer finish, making them suitable for different brand styles and price points.
Are velvet jewellery boxes sold in trade packs or wholesale quantities?
Yes, these boxes are intended for trade and wholesale buyers. Pack quantities, pricing visibility and stock status can vary by product, so checking the individual product page is the best way to confirm ordering units, current availability and delivery timing for repeat purchases.
Can velvet jewellery boxes be customised with a logo?
