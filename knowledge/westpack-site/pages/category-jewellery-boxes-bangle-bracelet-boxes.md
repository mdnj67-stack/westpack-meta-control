Choose bracelet boxes and bangle boxes by fit and format
Different jewellery styles need different box proportions. Long rectangular bracelet boxes are often the best choice for chain bracelets and slim cuffs because they keep the product centred and reduce movement. Square or deeper constructions are often a better fit for rigid bangles, chunkier cuffs and some bracelet watches that need more internal width or depth.
Bangle boxes are useful when a standard bracelet box feels too narrow or shallow, especially for rigid shapes that need more internal clearance. Check the listed dimensions carefully and look at insert design as well as outer size. A box with the right insert can improve presentation and protection for delicate bracelets, while wider inserts or deeper cavities help accommodate broader jewellery formats without forcing the lid or distorting the display.

Frequently asked questions about bracelet boxes
Below are answers to common trade questions about fit, wholesale ordering, branding and lead times for bracelet boxes.
Which bracelet box is best for chain bracelets, cuffs or bangles?
Chain bracelets usually work best in slimmer rectangular bracelet boxes with an insert that keeps the piece centred. Wider cuffs may need a broader internal fit, while rigid bangles often suit deeper or squarer bangle boxes that give more room across the width and height. If you sell several bracelet types, ordering samples is the easiest way to compare fit before placing a larger trade order.
Can I order bracelet boxes in bulk or wholesale quantities?
Yes. Westpack supplies wholesale bracelet boxes and bracelet boxes bulk ordering options for trade buyers in the UK. This suits jewellers, retailers, brands and resellers that need anything from smaller repeat runs to larger volume orders across multiple locations.
Can I add my logo to bracelet gift boxes?
Yes. Branded bracelet gift boxes are available as a core buying option, not just a small add-on. Printing methods depend on the box style and finish, with options such as foil printing available on suitable products. Minimum quantities apply to logo printed orders, and lead times are normally longer than for plain stock boxes.
How do I choose the right box size, depth and insert?
Start with the jewellery type and its widest point. Slim chain bracelets usually need less depth, while chunkier bangles and cuffs often need deeper constructions and wider inserts. Inserts affect both presentation and protection, so check whether the insert holds the piece securely without compressing it. Use the listed dimensions as your main guide and request samples if the fit is critical.
What are the lead times for plain stock and branded bracelet boxes?
Plain stock lines are the fastest option and are suitable when you need quick dispatch. Branded bracelet boxes take longer because they include print production and approval steps, and timing can vary by product and order size. If you are working to a deadline, it can be practical to order plain stock now and plan a printed run for the next cycle.
