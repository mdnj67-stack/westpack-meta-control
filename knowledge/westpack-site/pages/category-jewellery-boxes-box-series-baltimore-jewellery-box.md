Elegant Design with Practical Functionality
Crafted to balance beauty and practicality, the Baltimore jewellery box delivers on both fronts. The velour finish not only exudes luxury but also protects delicate items from scratches. Its sturdy construction means it’s perfect for jewellery boxes designed for frequent handling in store displays or during transit.
Customisation for Brand Enhancement
Brand identity is crucial in the jewellery business, and the Baltimore series allows for effortless jewellery box series branding with bespoke logo print inside the lid. This feature transforms each box into a silent brand ambassador, reinforcing your identity every time a piece is presented to a customer.
Optimised for Professional Settings
These Baltimore jewellery boxes are tailored for B2B use, specifically catering to jewellery stores, goldsmiths, and designers. With a focus on bulk buying, these boxes come with the advantage of no setup costs and low minimum order quantities, aligning with the business needs for efficient and economical procurement.
Broad Range and Expert Support
The Baltimore series offers versatility, with a range designed to accommodate everything from rings to necklaces. Our user-centric approach is supported by our outstanding customer service, ready to provide guidance and support, ensuring that your purchase experience meets professional standards.
