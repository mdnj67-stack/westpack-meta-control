Branded gift packaging is one of the most effective ways to turn every purchase into a professional brand experience. Whether you run a jewellery shop, fashion boutique, cosmetic clinic, or an online store, coordinated packaging with your logo helps you stand out, enhance perceived value, and keep customers coming back. At Westpack, we specialise in helping B2B clients create a consistent, memorable unboxing experience across every sales channel.
Branded gift packaging that reinforces your brand
With branded gift packaging, your logo becomes part of the story your customers take home. Our range includes printed gift boxes, gift bags with logo, and matching accessories that create a coherent look from counter to carrier bag. The result is professional presentation that feels thoughtful and carefully planned.
We offer a wide assortment of sizes and formats, making it easy to work with everything from delicate jewellery and watches to accessories, cosmetics, and lifestyle products. Our designers ensure your logo looks perfect, advising on placement, print colour, and production options so your packaging reflects your visual identity accurately.
Printed gift boxes, wholesale gift boxes, and flat pack solutions
If you work with higher volumes, you will appreciate our selection of wholesale gift boxes and flat pack boxes. They are designed for B2B use, with practical construction and durable materials that stand up to daily handling in-store and in your warehouse. For shops and webshops that need flexible storage, flat-pack constructions are particularly space-saving.
You can combine your branded gift packaging with coordinated flat-pack gift boxes to streamline your packing routine. Many retailers also supplement their packaging set-up with magnetic boxes with logo, which offer an elegant, rigid solution ideal for premium products, corporate gifts, and seasonal campaigns.
Gift bags with logo, luxury gift bags, and finishing touches
Gift bags with logo are a simple but powerful way to get your brand seen in the street. Our luxury gift bags are designed to look exclusive and feel comfortable to carry, making them perfect for jewellery stores, fashion retailers, and high-end gift shops. The strong handles and well-balanced formats ensure both function and aesthetics.
For a complete presentation, pair your gift bags with coordinated branded wrapping paper and elegant gift ribbons . These details elevate your branded gift packaging and help you create a recognisable signature style that customers associate with quality and attention to detail.
Gift card boxes, magnetic boxes with logo, and branded wrapping paper
Gift cards are often purchased for special occasions, so presenting them in dedicated gift card boxes instantly makes them feel more substantial and thoughtful. Our range of gift card boxes is developed specifically for professional use, with formats that fit standard gift card sizes and allow space for small inserts or message cards.
Complement your boxes with magnetic boxes with logo and branded wrapping paper to create a versatile toolbox of packaging options. Westpack offers small batches and large volumes, so you can test new designs or support campaigns without overstocking. As packaging experts since 1953, we are ready to guide you towards the most efficient, brand-boosting solution for your business.
FAQ
Find quick answers to common questions about our branded gift packaging.
Can I print my logo on the gift boxes and bags?
Yes, you can have your logo printed on many of our printed gift boxes and gift bags with logo. Our in-house Logo Print specialists work with hot foil stamping and other techniques to achieve a sharp, professional result. Logo print is aimed at professional B2B customers and helps your branded gift packaging create a consistent visual identity across boxes, bags, and accessories.
What is the delivery time for branded gift packaging?
Production of branded gift packaging involves several steps, including preparing your artwork, setting up print tools, and quality checks before dispatch. Because of this, delivery time can vary depending on product type and order size. Our customer service team will always inform you of the expected schedule in your order confirmation, and you can contact us for guidance when planning seasonal campaigns or product launches.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
The minimum order quantity (MOQ) for branded gift packaging depends on the specific product, such as printed gift boxes, gift card boxes, or luxury gift bags. We focus on low minimum order quantities on many items to make it easier for independent shops, chains, and webshops to get started. Detailed MOQ information is shown on each product page, and our team is happy to advise you on suitable quantities.
Do you offer eco-friendly gift wrapping options?
We continuously develop our assortment of gift wrapping and branded gift packaging to meet professional market demands. While we offer a wide range of materials, designs, and finishes, we recommend checking the individual product descriptions for specific information about materials and certifications. Our team can help you put together a coherent concept with printed gift boxes, branded wrapping paper, and matching bags.
Can I order a sample before buying?
Yes, in many cases you can Order a sample to review the look and feel of your branded gift packaging before placing a larger order. This is especially useful when you are choosing between several printed gift boxes, magnetic boxes with logo, or different finishes. Ordering a sample allows you to test the packaging with your products and ensure it fits your brand and workflow in store or in your packing area.
