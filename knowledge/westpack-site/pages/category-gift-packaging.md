Gift boxes, gift bags and gift wrapping for business use
Boxes and bags each suit different jobs. Gift boxes are usually the better choice when the product needs structure, insert compatibility or a stronger presentation for premium items such as jewellery, watches and gift cards. Paper gift bags work well for quick retail packing, lighter items and shops offering a fast gift wrapping service at the till.
If you are choosing between magnetic and fold-flat boxes, magnetic boxes give a more substantial presentation and a secure closure for corporate gifting or higher-value retail lines. Fold-flat boxes save storage space and can be more practical when you need larger volumes on hand. Rigid boxes are a strong option when finish and presentation quality matter most, while folding styles like pillow boxes suit businesses balancing storage, assembly time and stock flexibility.
Gift packaging for jewellery, watches, gift cards and small accessories
Jewellery and other small premium items need packaging that fits closely and presents cleanly. Jewellery gift boxes with box inserts for jewellery and small items help hold rings, earrings, necklaces and bracelets in place, while watch boxes and gift card packaging give a more tailored fit for those formats. Getting the right size avoids excess movement and helps the product look considered when opened.
When selecting sizes, think about both the product and the presentation extras. A ring box needs a compact insert-led format, while a necklace or bracelet may need more depth or length for safe placement. For small accessories, choose a box or bag with enough room for tissue paper, tags or stickers without making the pack look oversized. Colour options and finish choices also matter if you want packaging to align across product ranges or seasonal merchandising.

Frequently asked questions about gift packaging
Below are answers to common commercial questions about choosing, branding and reordering gift packaging for business use.
What gift packaging is best for jewellery products?
For jewellery, gift boxes are usually the best choice because they offer structure, better presentation and insert compatibility. Jewellery gift boxes with fitted inserts work well for rings, earrings, necklaces and bracelets, while watch boxes and gift card formats are better for those specific items. If you need a quicker retail handover, you can pair the box with a matching paper gift bag, tissue paper and ribbon.
Should I use gift bags or gift boxes for retail gifting?
Use gift bags when speed, easy storage and quick retail packing matter most. Use gift boxes when the product needs more protection, a closer fit or a stronger presentation. For many retailers, bags suit lighter items and counter sales, while boxes are better for jewellery, watches, gift cards and small premium accessories. A box can also be placed inside a bag when you want both structure and convenient carry-out packaging.
What is the difference between rigid, folding and magnetic gift boxes?
Rigid gift boxes are made for a more solid presentation and are often chosen for premium retail packaging. Folding gift boxes store flat, which helps when space is limited or larger quantities are needed. Magnetic gift boxes add a built-in closure and are often used for corporate gifting or higher-value presentation. The right choice depends on your storage space, packing process, product value and the finish you want to achieve.
Can I order gift packaging in bulk and reorder the same lines later?
Yes. Westpack supplies gift packaging for wholesale and repeat business ordering, with pack size options suited to regular trade use. Buying in volume can lower unit cost, and consistent stock availability helps businesses reorder the same packaging line when they need to maintain a uniform presentation across stores, websites or ongoing campaigns.
Can I match boxes with tissue paper, ribbon, tags and stickers?
Yes. Many businesses build a coordinated set using boxes or paper gift bags with tissue paper, ribbon and bows, tags and stickers to create a consistent finished look. You can also add branded elements such as logo printed packaging, printed ribbon or custom stickers. Matching accessories are useful for jewellers, boutiques, online sellers and corporate gifting teams that want packaging to look consistent across every order.
