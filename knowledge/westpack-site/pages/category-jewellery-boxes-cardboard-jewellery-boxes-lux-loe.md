Jewellery boxes in classic design with a twist
The London ECO jewellery boxes are luxurious boxes. The jewellery box is available in several different sizes and colours . Each jewellery box has a matching piece of protective paper as extra protection for the jewellery piece. Whether you are looking for an elegant ring box, an earring box, or a box for jewellery sets, you will find the jewellery box in this series. The London ECO jewellery boxes stand out from the crowd with their "sandwich look" . The box consists of a base and a classic "lift-off" lid + an intermediate piece. The intermediate piece helps give the box more height and of course a delightful twist in the design . The jewellery box is made from FSC®-certified paper and water-based glue. All in all, a truly elegant jewellery box for your high-end jewellery.
