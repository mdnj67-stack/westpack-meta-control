Low jewellery boxes perfect for shipping
The flat Frankfurt ECO jewellery boxes with a height of just 17mm are especially perfect if you sell many pieces of jewellery online and ship them to your customers. If you send one piece of jewellery at a time, you can place the box in one of our shipping bags or envelopes . If you send several jewellery boxes at once to the same customer, it is advantageous to place them in one of our postal boxes . The postal box can be printed with your logo either on the inside of the box or on the outside.
The classic design characterises the Frankfurt ECO series. The jewellery box has a lift-off lid. The lid goes down over the base, which makes the construction very stable. The foam insert inside the box has different cuts, meaning it fits many of your jewellery pieces. The jewellery box is Westpack’s lowest ECO shipping box.
These cardboard boxes are produced locally at our factory in Denmark, which helps ensure the lowest possible CO² emissions, as the jewellery box is not transported long distances. All our jewellery boxes are continuously tarnish tested.
A personalised logo print on the lid
Order your jewellery boxes with your personal logo printed on the box lid. Firstly, it looks incredibly stylish , secondly, it makes your customers remember you better , and thirdly, it is included in the price . Choose the print colour that best suits your personal branding and achieve a stylish and personal look . There are many print colours to choose from. If you are unsure which colour best suits your logo, order a sample first and decide afterwards once you have studied your sample carefully. It is important that you are satisfied with your personal logo .
Please contact our customer service if you have any questions.
