Choose the Right Branded Gift Bag
Start with the bag type and product fit. Small bags are commonly used for jewellery, watches and compact cosmetics. Medium formats suit candles, boxed beauty items and smaller gift sets. Large and extra large bags are better for apparel, larger presentation boxes and multi-item packs. Bottle bag options are available for wines, spirits, oils and other tall products. Many ranges are offered in portrait and landscape formats so you can match the bag shape to the contents and the preferred logo placement.
Paper gift bags with rope handles are often chosen for luxury presentation, boutique packaging and higher-value gift sets. Paper gift bags with twisted paper handles are a practical choice for everyday retail and promotional use. Luxury paper gift bags are ideal when finish and presentation are central to the buying experience. Available colours typically include kraft, white, black and selected coloured bag options, making it easier to align with brand guidelines and campaign themes.
Bag Sizes, Formats and Product Fit Guide
Check bag dimensions in mm before ordering to make sure your chosen format fits the product and any inner packaging. As a guide, jewellery usually suits compact formats such as around 90 x 50 x 120 mm to 150 x 70 x 160 mm. Cosmetics and small candles often fit medium sizes such as around 180 x 80 x 220 mm. Apparel and larger gift sets may require large or extra large bags such as around 250 x 100 x 300 mm, 320 x 120 x 400 mm or larger. Bottle bags are typically narrow portrait formats designed around single-bottle presentation.
If you are choosing bags for a product range rather than one item, it can be more efficient to standardise into two or three business gift bag sizes instead of ordering multiple niche formats. This supports easier stock control, more consistent branding and simpler repeat ordering across locations or campaigns.
Gift Bag Printing Options: Foil vs Digital Print
Foil print is a strong option for simple logos, monograms and text where you want a sharp, high-contrast finish on kraft, white, black or coloured bags. It is often selected for boutiques, jewellery packaging, premium beauty presentation and hospitality gifting. Digital or full-colour print is better when your artwork includes multiple colours, tonal detail, gradients or illustration work that foil cannot reproduce effectively.
Print suitability also depends on the bag construction and the available branding area. Some gift bags support a small print area for a discreet logo, while others allow a large print area or wider branding coverage for stronger visibility. Depending on the product, single-sided and double-sided print options may be available. If your team is comparing costs, lead times and visual impact, foil usually suits premium simplicity, while digital print suits brand-rich graphics and colour accuracy.
Print Placement, Artwork Setup and Approval Process
Logo placement should be planned around handle position, gussets and bag proportions. A centred front print works well for most portrait retail bags, while wider landscape formats may suit a lower or broader logo position. Not every bag type offers the same print area, so it is important to choose the bag first, then confirm whether your logo is best suited to a small print area, a large print area or double-sided branding. For bottle bags, print is usually positioned for clear front-facing presentation when carried.
For gift bag printing, artwork should be supplied in print-ready formats such as vector PDF, AI or EPS where possible, with fonts outlined and colours clearly specified. If digital print is selected, high-resolution files may also be suitable depending on the design. Westpack can provide downloadable templates or artwork guides and a print-ready checklist covering bleed, safe area, sizing and file setup. After upload or submission, artwork is checked, a proof or approval step is completed, and production begins once sign-off is confirmed. If artwork is not print-ready, our team can advise on the next steps.

FAQs About Customised Gift Bags
Below are answers to common buying questions about minimums, artwork, print methods, lead times and reordering for branded gift bags.
What is the minimum order quantity for branded gift bags?
Minimum order quantity depends on the bag type, size and print method. Standard online products usually show a clear starting quantity, while bespoke printed gift bags and higher-volume projects may require a quote. If you need volume pricing, the unit cost typically improves at quantity breaks, so it is worth comparing campaign demand, store rollout volumes or repeat usage before ordering.
How do I choose between foil print and digital print?
Choose foil print for simple logos, text and premium metallic presentation. It is often the best fit for luxury paper gift bags, boutique packaging and branding with one clean colour or finish. Choose digital or full-colour print when your logo includes multiple colours, gradients or fine detail. The right option depends on your artwork, budget, lead time and the bag type's available print area.
What bag size should I choose for jewellery, cosmetics, candles or gift sets?
Use the product dimensions plus any inner box, tissue paper or protective packaging to select the right bag size in mm. Small bags are usually suitable for jewellery and compact cosmetics, medium bags for candles and boxed beauty products, and large or extra large formats for apparel and gift sets. Bottle bag options are available for single-bottle presentation. If you are buying across a product range, standardising a few core sizes can simplify replenishment and reordering.
What happens after I upload my artwork?
Your artwork is reviewed against the chosen bag type, print method and print area. If needed, you may be asked to supply a vector file, adjust sizing or confirm colours. Westpack can provide downloadable templates or artwork guides to help prepare files correctly. Once the artwork approval process is completed and sign-off is confirmed, production and dispatch follow the agreed lead time.
Can I reorder the same business gift bags later?
Yes, repeat-order convenience is an important part of business buying. Reordering the same branded gift bags helps maintain brand consistency across stores, events, campaigns and seasonal gifting. If your artwork and specification remain unchanged, repeat orders are typically more straightforward to place. For larger repeat volumes, you can also request a bespoke quote to review current price breaks and delivery options.
