Ring boxes are a core packaging choice for jewellers, jewellery brands, gift shops, online retailers and retail chains that need reliable presentation for single rings, engagement rings, wedding bands and paired sets. Westpack supplies ring boxes in plain and branded formats, from entry-level cardboard styles to more premium velvet-look, flocked, leatherette and wood-effect finishes.
You can choose ring boxes by material, colour, insert style and intended use, then narrow your options further by presentation level and branding needs. Our range includes single ring boxes for standard retail presentation, double ring boxes for wedding bands and paired sets, plus branded ring boxes with logo printing , sample ordering, stock support and repeat-order guidance for trade buyers.
How to choose ring boxes
Choose ring boxes by ring type, insert fit, finish and branding needs. Single ring boxes suit standard retail presentation and engagement rings, while double ring boxes are better for wedding bands and paired sets. Plain stock is often the best fit for quick dispatch and everyday trade buying, while branded ring boxes help create a more consistent packaging line.

Single and double ring boxes for trade presentation
Our ring boxes are designed around real retail use. Single ring boxes suit solitaire rings, dress rings, engagement rings and standard band presentation, while double ring boxes provide a tidy way to present wedding bands, couple sets or paired rings in one format. This helps keep display, fulfilment and packaging presentation consistent across different collections.
Before clicking into a product, check the listed dimensions and insert layout so you can assess fit for slim bands, wider bands or taller settings. Inserts vary by style and may be better suited to solitaire heads, plain bands or paired rings, so choosing by interior fit is just as important as choosing by outer finish.
Wholesale ring boxes in plain stock and branded formats
Westpack supplies wholesale ring boxes for small independent jewellers through to larger retail chains. Plain stock options are a practical choice when you need fast dispatch, easy replenishment and an unbranded look that works across multiple product lines. They are also useful if you need ready-to-order packaging for resale, gifting or short-run promotions without committing to custom print.
If you want stronger brand recognition, branded ring boxes and ring boxes with logo are available for custom runs. Printing methods typically include foil for a sharp metallic or single-colour logo finish, and digital print where multi-colour artwork is needed. Branded orders usually involve artwork approval before production, which also makes repeat ordering easier once your packaging line is set up.
When sending ring boxes directly to customers, choose postal packaging for jewellery that protects the box and keeps the arrival experience tidy.
Ring boxes in cardboard, velvet-look, flocked and leatherette finishes
Material and finish affect both presentation and cost. Use the material filter to compare cardboard ring gift boxes with more premium rigid styles, depending on the ring type, presentation goal and price point. Cardboard styles are often the best fit for everyday retail and larger-volume use, while velvet-look and flocked finishes bring a softer jewellery presentation.
For a more structured or premium finish, buyers may also compare leatherette jewellery boxes , wooden jewellery boxes and plastic jewellery boxes . Colour choice matters too, especially if you want ring boxes to align with your brand palette, gift bag range or wider jewellery packaging. If you also need a coordinated presentation across boxes, pouches and bags, related categories such as jewellery boxes , jewellery pouches for rings and jewellery gift bags can support a more complete packaging set.
Branded ring boxes with logo: foil or digital print
For many brands, the main decision is not whether to print, but how. Foil print is usually the better choice for simple logos, monograms and small-position branding on lids, giving a crisp finish that works well on matte, glossy, leatherette and selected rigid box styles. Digital print is better when your artwork includes multiple colours or finer detail that would be difficult to achieve with foil.
Logo position depends on the box construction, but front-centre lid placement is the most common for ring boxes with logo. If you are building a branded packaging line, ordering samples or checking a proof first is the safest route, especially when you want colour matching and a consistent look across ring boxes, earring boxes , bangle and bracelet boxes , necklace boxes or even watch boxes for retailers .
Wholesale ring boxes: samples, lead times and repeat orders
Trade buyers usually need a clear route from first test order to ongoing supply. Samples help you check box size, insert tension, hinge action and surface finish before committing to volume. This is especially useful when comparing a budget line against a more premium box or testing whether a single or double format works better for your range.
Lead times are normally shorter for plain stock ring boxes and longer for custom printed orders, as branded production requires artwork approval and print setup. Once approved, repeat orders are easier to manage because the packaging specification is already established. That gives jewellers and retailers more confidence in consistency of supply across seasonal replenishment and core packaging lines.

FAQs about ring boxes
Below are answers to common trade questions about fit, branding, order quantities and reordering.
What is the difference between a single and double ring box?
A single ring box is made for one ring, usually a solitaire, signet, engagement ring or standard band. A double ring box has space for two rings and is commonly used for wedding bands, couple sets or paired presentation. The right choice depends on whether you are presenting one ring or two, and on the insert layout inside the box.
Can I order plain or empty ring box options without branding?
Yes. Plain stock and empty ring box options are available for businesses that want quick dispatch, lower minimum commitment or a neutral presentation across different jewellery lines. These are often used by jewellers, gift shops and ecommerce retailers that need ready-to-order packaging without custom print.
Do you offer branded ring boxes with logo printing?
Yes. Branded ring boxes are available with logo printing, with options such as foil print or digital print depending on the box style and artwork. Printed orders usually follow an artwork approval process, after which repeat orders are easier to place against the same packaging specification.
How do I choose the right ring box size and insert?
Start with the ring type. Slim bands, wider wedding bands and taller solitaire settings can all need different insert support and box depth. Check the product dimensions and interior layout before ordering, and if you are comparing styles for a new range, a sample order is the best way to confirm fit.
Do you offer wholesale ring boxes for small and large businesses?
Yes. The range is suitable for smaller independent businesses as well as larger-volume buyers. You can buy from stock for everyday needs, test samples before scaling up, and move into wholesale or branded runs when you need more consistent supply, custom presentation or easier repeat ordering.
