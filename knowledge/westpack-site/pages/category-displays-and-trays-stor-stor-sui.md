Sturdy suitcases and handy transport bags for your drawers and jewellery trays, with a neutral look.
For transporting many trays, Westpack has a classic model for rectangular jewellery trays (for 10-12 trays), and a light-weight version for square trays, designed especially for the travelling sales representatives (for 20-60 trays). The number of trays each suitcase can hold depends on their size.
If you travel with a small collection of jewellery, we recommend using our neutral canvas transport bag. This sturdy black bag with zip-lock can hold 6 trays. Both the lightweight and the glossily lacquered trays can be transported in this bag.
