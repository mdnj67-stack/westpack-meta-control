Printed Gift Boxes with Logo: Choose the Right Branding Method
Custom gift boxes with logo are often ordered to strengthen brand recall, improve shelf presentation and make premium products easier to position at a higher perceived value. Westpack offers logo print options including foil and digital/full-colour print . Foil is typically the right choice when you want a sharp, high-contrast logo, metallic effect or a clean premium finish on simpler artwork. Digital print is better when your design includes multiple colours, gradients, fine detail or fuller coverage.
The best print method depends on the artwork itself, the box material, the target look and your budget across the order quantity. We provide artwork templates and artwork approval support before production, helping trade buyers check placement, scale and fit. Sample availability can also reduce buying risk before committing to a larger branded packaging run.
Custom Gift Boxes by Style, Structure and Closure
The right box style depends on how the product is sold, packed and presented. Rigid boxes are a strong choice for premium retail presentation and higher-margin jewellery. Magnetic gift boxes work well for gift sets, corporate gifting and elevated unboxing. Drawer boxes offer a structured reveal and are often used for bracelets, watches and curated sets. Lid-and-base presentation boxes suit classic jewellery packaging, while flat-pack gift boxes can help businesses save storage space and improve packing efficiency. Postal gift boxes are useful when products need a presentation-led pack that also supports ecommerce shipment.
Westpack shows product dimensions clearly to help buyers compare internal fit, depth and footprint before ordering. Common use cases include compact formats for rings and earrings, mid-size boxes for necklaces and bracelets, deeper profiles for watches, and larger formats for gift sets or multi-item bundles. Many styles can be paired with inserts for better jewellery presentation and protection, including layouts designed to hold rings, earrings, necklaces and bracelets securely during display or transit.
How to Choose the Right Size, Depth and Insert Setup
Box size should be selected around the product, the insert and the intended presentation standard, not just the outer dimensions. Rings and stud earrings usually need compact boxes with a close-fitting insert to prevent movement. Necklaces often require more length and depth to accommodate chains and pendants neatly. Bracelet and watch boxes generally benefit from deeper cavities or padded insert options, while gift sets need enough internal space to separate multiple items cleanly.
If you are comparing formats across a range, start with the product dimensions, then review internal box measurements, insert thickness and closure type. A shallow rigid box may suit counter display, while a deeper magnetic or drawer format may be better for gifting upgrades or premium ecommerce orders. Westpack can help buyers align size, depth and insert compatibility so packaging looks proportionate, protects the contents and remains practical for packing teams.

FAQs About Custom Gift Boxes
Below are answers to common trade buying questions about custom gift boxes, branding options, order volumes, lead times and product fit.
What is the minimum order quantity for custom gift boxes?
Minimum order quantities depend on the box style, size and print method. Standard branded box options are often the best route if you need lower-volume ordering, while fully bespoke gift boxes usually require higher quantities. Westpack can advise on low MOQ options where relevant and provide pricing guidance for bulk orders.
What is the difference between foil print and digital print?
Foil print is usually best for simple logos, sharp linework and a premium metallic or high-contrast finish. Digital or full-colour print is better for multi-colour artwork, gradients, detailed graphics and broader design coverage. The right option depends on your artwork, the box material, the visual result you want and the order quantity.
Can I order samples before placing a larger order?
Yes, sample availability can help reduce buying risk before you commit to a larger order. Samples are useful for checking size, depth, structure, insert fit and overall presentation, especially when comparing rigid, magnetic, drawer or flat-pack box formats for jewellery or premium gift products.
How long do custom printed gift boxes take to produce?
Lead times vary by product, branding method and order size. Standard branded formats are generally faster to order than fully bespoke projects. Westpack can advise on current dispatch expectations, artwork approval timing and production lead times so you can plan launches, seasonal campaigns and replenishment orders more accurately.
Can I reorder the same branded box design later?
Yes, Westpack supports repeat orders for businesses that need consistent branded packaging across ongoing product lines or multiple order cycles. Reordering is easier when artwork has already been approved, and we can help maintain brand consistency across boxes, inserts and matching packaging accessories.
