Westpack’s environmental strategy
Internally and externally, we constantly raise the bar with regards to sustainability. We do this because many of our customers – from the small independent jewellery designers to the well-established jewellery brands – are working hard to make their businesses as sustainable as possible. And as a trusted supplier for these businesses, we feel obliged to place an equally strong focus on sustainability.
Internally
Because packaging only exists to protect, present, or store a primary product, it sometimes has a relatively short life-cycle. Therein lies the challenge we are constantly trying to solve: how do we develop and manufacture products that (despite the sometimes short life-cycles) are sustainable and eco-friendly?
Through decades of experience within the field, we have accumulated knowledge that is entirely unique to our company. This allows us to focus our environmental efforts wisely.
We use our focused efforts to constantly implement new environmental initiatives. And, just as important, we review the initiatives to find out what is working well and what should be improved. Finally, we measure our progress. This is crucial for having a transparent business.

Externally
Because our customers cannot be expected to have a vast knowledge about the environmental impact of packaging, we strive to educate. If requested, we happily take the time to have a dialog with our customers about eco-friendly packaging choices. We also focus on influencing our business partners. Hopefully, with time, this can inspire changes within the packaging industry. On a larger scale, we wish to contribute to the of the goals set forth in UNs Global Compact.
