Letterbox boxes are a practical choice for B2B buyers shipping jewellery, gift sets and other flat products through many standard UK letterboxes when packed to the right size. For jewellery brands, boutiques, subscription businesses, gift retailers and online shops, they help reduce failed deliveries while giving orders better protection and a more polished arrival than envelopes or mailing bags.
This range includes letterbox-friendly jewellery boxes, slim jewellery boxes and large letter packaging formats suited to necklaces that need a flatter alternative to standard necklace boxes , as well as bracelets, earrings, pendant cards, pouch-packed jewellery, gift vouchers, small accessories, curated gift packs and flat gift sets. Business benefits of letterbox boxes
Reduce missed deliveries
Boxes designed for letterbox use can help more orders arrive on the first attempt, which is useful for ecommerce dispatch, subscription packs and campaign mailings. They are also a strong fit for Royal Mail large letter packaging where packed size and weight stay within current limits.
Protect delicate and presentation-led products
Compared with envelopes or mailing bags , a structured box gives better support for earrings, bracelets, necklace cards, voucher packs and other delicate items that need to arrive flat, secure and ready to open. Add inserts, tissue, shredded fill, pouches or sleeves for extra hold in transit.
Support repeat trade ordering
Stocked lines make it easier to reorder familiar sizes for ongoing dispatch, while bulk pack sizes and quantity breaks support larger runs. If you need a branded version, Westpack can also help with logo print options, artwork support and lead time guidance for repeatable branded packaging.
Letterbox boxes for jewellery and gift retailers
These letterbox boxes are built around the needs of trade buyers sending small products by post without losing the presentation value of the pack. They work well for jewellery brands sending necklace sets, bracelet cards, earrings, pendant cards and pouch-packed pieces, as well as gift retailers packing vouchers, flat accessories, curated gift packs and event mailers.
For many businesses, the appeal is the balance of postal practicality and brand presentation. A slim postal format can still look gift-ready when paired with tissue paper, ribbon, branded stickers, inserts or jewellery pouches and bags , making it suitable for ecommerce gifting, boutique launches, influencer mailers and subscription fulfilment.
Royal Mail large letter packaging and letterbox box sizing
If you are buying for large letter postal delivery, box choice should be based on the final packed item, not the outer box alone. A slim jewellery box with a necklace card may stay within large letter parameters, while the same box with tissue, ribbon and a thicker insert may move into small parcel territory. Always check your final packed dimensions and weight against current Royal Mail limits before committing to a format.
A practical way to buy is by matching the box profile to the product inside. Small square and A6 formats often suit earrings and bracelets, especially when a flatter postal format is preferred to standard earring boxes or bangle / bracelet boxes , along with vouchers and pendant cards. DL and A5 letterbox boxes can be better for necklace sets, flat gift sets, invitation packs or curated assortments. Larger large-letter formats can help when you need more presentation area while keeping the depth low.
Letterbox-friendly jewellery boxes vs gift boxes and postal boxes
Use letterbox-friendly jewellery boxes when the priority is posting slim products efficiently through standard UK letterboxes with a clean branded presentation. They are a good fit for flatter items where depth needs to stay tight but the pack still needs structure and visual impact.
Choose gift boxes & bags when in-store presentation or a more substantial keepsake format matters more than postal dimensions. Choose standard postal boxes or mailing cartons when the product is deeper, more fragile or part of a larger multi-item order. This comparison helps buyers decide whether they need a slim jewellery box, a presentation box or a deeper shipping carton for dispatch protection.
Letterbox boxes with closures, finishes and branding options
Different constructions suit different packing workflows. Postal lock styles are useful for quick hand packing and secure closure. Peel-and-seal formats can speed up packing benches for higher-volume dispatch. Rigid foldable formats are a good option when you want a more gift-led feel without moving straight to a full presentation box.
Depending on the assortment, you can choose plain white or brown boxes for immediate use, or move to logo printed letterbox boxes for a stronger unboxing experience. For branded projects, it helps to plan for artwork approval, print setup and lead times. If you are comparing options, ask for a sample or start with a plain stock line first, then move to printed packaging once the size and fit are confirmed.
Bulk letterbox boxes, inserts and repeat ordering support
For trade buyers, buying the right box is only part of the job. You also need dependable stock visibility, practical pack quantities and a simple route for repeat orders. Stocked lines are useful for fast dispatch and short lead-time replenishment, while bulk ordering and quantity breaks can support campaign launches, subscription runs and ongoing ecommerce fulfilment.
To complete the pack, pair your letterbox boxes with jewellery inserts, tissue paper , ribbon, stickers, shredded fill, pouches or sleeves depending on the item being shipped. Internal fit matters, especially for delicate products, so it is worth checking insert compatibility and how much room add-ons take up once packed. If you are testing a new format, ordering samples first is a low-risk way to confirm fit before placing a larger trade order.

FAQ: Buying letterbox boxes for business use
Here are some of the most common questions from business buyers choosing letterbox boxes for jewellery and gift postal delivery.
Are these letterbox boxes suitable for Royal Mail large letter delivery?
Many styles are designed with large letter postal delivery in mind, but suitability depends on the final packed dimensions and total weight of your product, insert and finishing materials. Always check your completed pack against current Royal Mail limits before dispatch.
Which letterbox box size is best for necklaces, bracelets, earrings or gift vouchers?
That depends on how the item is presented. Small square and A6 sizes often work for earrings, bracelets, pendant cards and vouchers. DL and A5 formats can be better for necklace cards, flat gift sets and curated presentation packs. If you are adding tissue, pouches or inserts, check the packed depth as well as the footprint.
What is the difference between a letterbox box and a standard postal box?
A letterbox box is made for slim, low-profile packing so it can pass through a standard UK letterbox when packed correctly. A standard postal box or mailing carton is usually deeper and better suited to bulkier products, multi-item shipments or orders that need more void fill and transit protection.
Are logo printed letterbox boxes available, and what are the order implications?
Yes. Trade customers can choose plain stock boxes for immediate use or request logo printed versions for branded shipping. Printed orders usually involve artwork approval, minimum quantities and lead times that are longer than stocked plain lines, so it is worth confirming sizing with samples before moving to print.
Can I order samples and then move to bulk quantities later?
Yes, that is often the best route if you are checking fit for jewellery, vouchers or flat gift sets. Start with samples or a smaller trial order, confirm how the product packs with inserts or tissue, then move to bulk quantities for repeat purchasing once the format is proven.
