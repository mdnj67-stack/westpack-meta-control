Business benefits of the right earring displays
Choose displays by product type
Different earrings need different presentation. Stud earring displays suit single pairs, carded studs and compact multi-pair layouts, while taller stands and open formats work better for long drops, hooks and pendant styles that need hanging space and visibility.
Match capacity to your selling space
Compact counter displays keep key lines visible without taking over limited space, while taller feature stands and higher-capacity options help you show a wider assortment in windows, showrooms and exhibition settings. Clear dimensions and pair counts make it easier to plan your layout before you order.
Keep ordering simple across your range

That makes it easier to reorder matching earring holder stands, add coordinated ring displays , necklace displays and bracelet displays , and build a consistent in-store presentation.
Earring display stands for shops, counters and showrooms
The right earring display stands for shop use depend on where the stock will be shown and how customers browse it. Lower profile formats are useful for countertops, till areas and glass cabinets where you want clean sightlines and quick access. Taller stands are better for feature zones, showroom styling, collection launches and window presentation where height helps draw attention from a distance.
If you sell at markets, exhibitions or trade fairs, lightweight stands, trays and acrylic holders are often the most practical choice because they are easy to position, move and reset. For product photography or studio presentation, simple shapes in black, white or clear finishes help keep the focus on the jewellery while still giving a polished retail look.
Stud earring displays and stands for drops, hoops and huggies
Choose your display format by earring type first. Stud earring displays are best when you need neat spacing for single pairs or small curated sets, especially for fine jewellery, new launches or bestselling lines. Ear-shaped displays, blocks and trays can also work well for studs and carded earrings where front-facing presentation matters.
For drop earrings, hook earrings, hoop earrings, huggies and pendant styles, look for earring display stands with enough height and clearance to stop pieces overlapping or touching the surface below. T-bars, trees and ladder formats are useful when you need to separate styles by length, metal tone or collection, while still keeping the display easy to browse.
Earring holder stands by size, height and display capacity
Capacity is one of the main buying decisions. A single-pair display is ideal for hero presentation, premium pieces and focal points in a cabinet or window. Multi-pair earring holder stands are better for broader assortment display, whether you want to show a few coordinated designs or a larger working range that supports fast stock turn.
Check the footprint as well as the overall height. Small-base displays suit compact counters and pop-up spaces, while taller formats make better use of vertical space in larger retail settings. It also helps to compare the number of pairs each stand holds and whether it is better suited to short earrings or longer styles that need more drop. This makes product selection more practical than choosing on appearance alone.
Materials and colours for retail-ready earring displays
Material choice changes the look of the jewellery and the feel of the display area. Clear acrylic displays give a clean, modern presentation and work well for busy retail spaces where visibility matters. Suede-look, leatherette displays and silk-look finishes create a softer presentation that suits finer ranges, gift-led collections and showroom appointments. Metal-style finishes can add structure and contrast for contemporary merchandising.
Colour is just as important. Black is often used to create contrast with bright metals and stones, white gives a lighter presentation for clean branding, and clear keeps the focus fully on the product. Depending on the range, other presentation-led shades can help you align displays with seasonal collections, brand colours or a specific in-store look without losing consistency across the category.

Frequently asked questions about earring displays
Here are answers to common trade questions about choosing the right earring displays for retail use, assortments and repeat ordering.
Which earring displays are best for stud earrings?
Stud earring displays are usually best in formats that keep pairs evenly spaced and front-facing, such as ear-shaped displays, blocks, trays and compact stands. For hero lines, a single-pair stand works well. For a broader retail assortment, choose multi-pair displays that let customers compare several styles at once without crowding.
What is the best display stand for long drop earrings?
Long drop earrings need enough height and clearance to hang freely. Taller earring display stands, T-bars, trees and ladder-style displays are usually the best option because they stop pieces from touching the base or overlapping with nearby pairs. Check the product dimensions carefully so the stand suits the length of your range.
How many pairs of earrings can each display hold?
Capacity varies by format. Some displays are designed for a single pair to create a focal point, while others hold a small edited selection or a larger multi-pair assortment. The best choice depends on your stock depth, counter space and whether the display is for hero presentation, everyday retail selling or exhibition use.
Which earring holder stands work best for shops with limited counter space?
For smaller counters, look for earring holder stands with a compact footprint and good vertical use of space. Slim stands, small trays and narrow multi-pair displays are often the most practical because they keep jewellery visible without blocking sightlines or taking over the counter.
Are there wholesale or bulk earring display stands available?
Yes. Westpack supports business ordering with wholesale-friendly and bulk ordering options across the range. You can review pack sizes, compare display formats for different store needs and reorder matching stands when you need to expand a display scheme, replace units or fit out additional locations.
