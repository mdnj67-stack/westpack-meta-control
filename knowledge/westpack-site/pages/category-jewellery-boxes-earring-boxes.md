Earring boxes are a practical packaging choice for jewellers, jewellery brands, gift shops and resellers that need reliable presentation for retail, gifting and day-to-day stock packaging. Westpack supplies earring boxes in plain and branded formats, with options for different earring types, box styles, inserts, colours and print finishes.
Choose from slim earring boxes, hinged boxes, lid-and-base boxes, shoulder boxes, drawer-style boxes and ribboned gift boxes designed for trade use. Whether you need wholesale earring boxes for fast-moving lines, logo-printed packaging for a more consistent retail presentation or stock jewellery boxes with clear dispatch expectations, this category helps you compare fit, finish, pack quantities and print suitability more quickly.
Business benefits of choosing the right earring boxes
Match the box to the earring type
Studs, hoops, drops, long earrings and earring-plus-pendant sets need different depths, inserts and internal support. Choosing by product fit helps reduce movement in transit, improves presentation at the point of sale and makes packing more consistent across your range.
Order plain stock now or plan branded packaging
Plain stock boxes are useful for urgent replenishment, testing new lines and everyday packing. Printed earring gift boxes are better for established collections, gift-led retail and repeat orders where logo visibility and consistent brand presentation matter.
Build a coordinated packaging range
Many earring boxes sit within matching jewellery packaging collections, making it easier to align earrings with bracelet boxes , ring boxes and necklace boxes .
Choose earring boxes by earring type and insert fit
The best earring boxes depend on what you are packing. Stud earrings usually work best in compact boxes with inserts that keep pairs centred, protect the posts and stop movement during handling. Hoops often need more internal clearance, especially for thicker profiles, while drop earrings and long earrings benefit from deeper boxes that prevent contact with the lid and reduce tangling during handling.
If you sell small pendant earrings or earring-plus-pendant sets, look at formats with extra depth or more flexible inserts so one box can cover multiple SKUs. Slim earring boxes are useful for lightweight, lower-profile styles and for ranges where compact shelf presentation matters. For more delicate pieces, pay attention to padding, slit position and insert structure so the jewellery stays upright and protected from packing bench to customer handover.
Wholesale earring boxes in plain stock and branded options
For trade buyers, the main decision is often plain stock versus printed packaging. Plain earring boxes are usually the fastest route for restocking, seasonal peaks, new product launches and lower-risk trial runs. They are also practical if you need flexibility across multiple jewellery lines or want to hold stock in staple colours such as black, white, grey, kraft or navy.
Branded earring gift boxes are better suited to repeatable retail presentation and stronger brand recognition. Depending on the box style, logo options may include foil branding or digital print. Foil works well for crisp logo placement on established ranges, while digital print can suit more detailed artwork or shorter-run projects where available. If you are comparing earring gift boxes wholesale, check minimums, print suitability by box family and typical lead times so you can plan branded orders alongside everyday stock supply.
Earring gift boxes in hinged, drawer, shoulder and lid-and-base styles
Different box families suit different retail outcomes. Hinged earring gift boxes offer a familiar presentation for counter sales and higher-value pieces. Drawer-style or matchbox formats are compact and easy to stack, making them useful for mail order, resale packing and neat stock control. Lid and base boxes are straightforward for everyday use, while shoulder boxes give a more substantial look for premium collections.
Ribboned gift boxes and luxury earring jewellery boxes are often chosen when presentation is part of the selling point, such as branded collections, gifting ranges or in-store display sets. Budget and everyday stock packaging is better when speed, volume and cost control matter most. The right choice depends on how the box will be used: retail display, back-of-house stock packing, online order fulfilment or coordinated in-store presentation across a wider jewellery range.
Material choice also affects both presentation and price point. For example, plastic jewellery boxes and cardboard jewellery boxes are often practical for everyday stock packaging, while leatherette or velour jewellery boxes and wooden jewellery boxes are better suited to more premium presentation.
Choose the right box size, insert and pack quantity
Size selection should be based on both the jewellery and the insert. Check external dimensions for shelf fit and packing workflow, but also the internal depth and insert style so the earrings sit correctly without bending posts, catching backs or shifting in transit. For studs, a compact insert may be enough. For hoops and drop earrings, allow more height and clearance. If one collection includes mixed styles, choosing a slightly more versatile box can simplify stock holding.
If you are sourcing earring boxes UK wide, clear stock visibility, sensible pack sizes and reliable dispatch matter just as much as the presentation itself. Earring boxes bulk ordering is useful for established ranges, repeat sellers and businesses standardising packaging across stores or product lines. Samples or low-risk trial ordering can help you confirm colour, fit and print suitability before committing to larger wholesale earring boxes volumes.
Printed earring boxes with matching jewellery packaging collections
If you want a cohesive packaging system, look beyond standalone boxes. Many earring boxes are part of matching collections across rings, pendants, bracelets and necklaces, which helps create display consistency in-store and a more recognisable brand experience online. This is especially useful for jewellers and resellers that carry multiple jewellery categories but want one clear packaging look.
To complete the range, pair your earring boxes with small jewellery bags , gift bags , carrier bags, tissue paper, replacement inserts , ribbons and postal packaging. This makes it easier to build a practical packing workflow around your chosen box family and colour palette, whether you prefer black, white, grey, kraft, pink, blue, green or navy.
If you are sending earrings on cards or in a low-profile presentation pack, letterbox jewellery boxes can help keep the order slim, protected and gift-ready.

FAQs about earring boxes
Here are answers to common trade questions about fit, branding, ordering and choosing the right earring boxes for your range.
Which earring boxes are best for stud earrings?
Stud earrings are usually best in compact boxes with a secure insert that holds the pair neatly in place. Look for shallow or slim formats if the design is small and lightweight, but check that the insert keeps the earrings centred and the backs protected.
What is the difference between plain and branded earring gift boxes?
Plain stock boxes are the practical choice for urgent orders, everyday packing and testing new lines. Branded earring gift boxes add logo visibility and are better for established retail presentation, repeat orders and ranges where consistent brand recognition is important.
How do I choose the right box size and insert for my earrings?
Start with the earring type. Studs need less depth than hoops, drops or long earrings. Check the box dimensions, internal clearance and insert style together, especially if the jewellery is delicate or has a longer drop. If one box needs to cover earrings and small pendant sets, choose a format with more flexible internal space.
Do you offer wholesale earring boxes and bulk pack quantities?
Yes. The range is suitable for both smaller business orders and higher-volume trade purchasing. If you are buying wholesale earring boxes or planning earring boxes bulk orders, review pack quantities, stock availability and dispatch timings so you can align purchasing with your sales cycle.
Which earring boxes can be printed with a logo and what are the lead times?
Many box styles can be branded, but print suitability varies by construction and finish. Common options include foil branding and digital print where available. Lead times depend on the chosen box family, quantity and artwork requirements, so it is worth checking the product details or requesting advice before placing a printed order.
