How to tie christmas ribbon?
Tying a christmas ribbon can enhance the presentation of your packages. Begin by measuring enough ribbon to wrap around the box twice. Tie a simple knot, then form two loops to create a bow, adjusting for symmetry. Finish by trimming the ends for a neat look.
