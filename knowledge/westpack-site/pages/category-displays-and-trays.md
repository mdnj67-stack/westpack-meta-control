Displays and trays for retail presentation
Organise products clearly
Display trays and inserts help keep products grouped, visible and easy to handle. They are useful for counters, showcases, sales appointments, stock-backed displays and trade fair setups where products need to stay tidy while customers browse.
Support shop floor and back-of-house use
Some displays are chosen for customer-facing presentation, while trays are often used for handling, sorting, replenishment and storage between sales moments. Choosing the right format helps staff work faster and keeps the presentation consistent throughout the day.
Connect displays with packaging
Westpack makes it easier to coordinate retail presentation with packaging, boxes and bags. That helps create a more consistent customer experience from display to final purchase.
Display trays for counters, showcases and sales appointments
Display trays are a practical choice when several products need to be presented together in a compact space. They work well on retail counters, inside glass cabinets, during sales appointments and at trade events where products are handled often and need to stay organised.
Trays can also support stock-backed selling, where staff need quick access to extra sizes, colours or styles while keeping the front-of-store presentation clean. For jewellery retailers, trays can be paired with dedicated display stands for rings, earrings, bracelets and necklaces.
Choose trays, inserts and display formats by use
The right display format depends on how the product will be used. Trays and inserts are useful for grouped assortments, sorting and handling. Pads and low-profile displays work well for compact counters. Raised stands, busts and holders are better when individual pieces need more visibility.

FAQ: Choosing displays and trays
Below are some of the most common trade questions buyers ask when comparing display trays, retail displays and coordinated presentation options.
What is the difference between displays and trays?
Displays are usually used to raise, hold or present individual products so they are easier to see. Trays are better for grouping, organising, handling and presenting several items together in a compact space. Many retailers use both in the same counter or showcase setup.
When should I use display trays?
Display trays are useful for counters, sales appointments, stock-backed selling, trade fairs and showroom use. They help keep products organised while making it easy for staff to handle, compare and replenish items during the day.
Which displays should I choose for jewellery?
For jewellery-specific presentation, choose the display type by product. Rings usually need holders, cones, pads or trays. Earrings need stands, trees or cards. Bracelets and bangles often need bars, rolls or trays, while necklaces and pendants need busts or taller stands. You can find these options in the jewellery displays category.
Can displays and trays be matched with packaging?
Yes. Many trade buyers match displays and trays with jewellery boxes, bags and branded packaging to create a consistent presentation from counter display to final purchase.
Are displays and trays suitable for trade fairs and pop-up selling?
Yes. Trays, compact stands and lightweight display formats are useful for trade fairs, exhibitions and pop-up retail because they are easy to move, reset and pack away between events.
