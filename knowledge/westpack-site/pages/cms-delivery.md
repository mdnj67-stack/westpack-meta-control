Delivery & Freight Prices
Orders will be dispatched to the Billing Address indicated by you during the Checkout process unless delivery to a different address is requested. We send you an e-mail with shipping details and a tracking number when your order has left the warehouse.
Please ensure that the delivery details we hold for you are correct. We ask you to inform us of any changes, prior to dispatch. Changes made after the parcel has been dispatch may result in a longer delivery time.
All items are delivered via a courier service. All parcels require a signature for delivery, which excludes deliveries to a PO-box from our delivery options. If all items are in stock, your order will usually be delivered as listed below.
Westpack is also VAT Registered in the UK
Ordering from the UK, you can place your order with Westpack without any concerns. We are registered for Value Added Tax (VAT) in the UK, which means you don't need to worry about any customs charges when making a purchase from us.
We handle all of it, and you only need to pay the standard UK VAT when placing your order.
Shop with confidence knowing that we take care of all the necessary procedures, and you won't encounter any unexpected fees or hassles.
