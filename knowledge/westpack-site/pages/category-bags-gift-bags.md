Gift bags by size, format and handle type
Start with dimensions. Gift bag sizes are normally shown as width x depth x height, so width is the front opening, depth is the gusset from front to back, and height is the measurement from base to top. This makes it easier to choose a bag that fits the product cleanly without too much empty space or a tight opening.
For format, portrait bags are useful for most boxed jewellery and accessories, landscape bags can suit wider gift sets, square bags work well for balanced presentation, and bottle bags are the right choice for narrow tall items. Handle choice also matters. Rope handles usually suit luxury gift bags and higher-end retail presentation, while twisted paper handles offer a practical option for everyday retail gift bags and heavier day-to-day use.
Gift bags for jewellery boxes, accessories and gift sets
Small gift bags for jewellery are often the right fit for ring boxes, earring boxes and compact bracelet boxes. Medium sizes can suit necklace boxes, larger bracelet boxes, watches and boxed accessories. Larger formats are useful when customers buy several items together or when you want to add tissue paper , ribbon or inserts for a fuller presentation.
If you are choosing for jewellery retail, look at both the box footprint and the bag depth. A ring box may need very little depth, while a necklace box or watch box often needs more room at the base. For multi-item purchases, a square or wider portrait format can help you pack two or more boxes neatly without crushing tissue or overfilling the bag. For retailers selling eyewear accessories, branded presentation can also include branded glasses cases.
Gift shops and jewellery retailers can also combine gift bags with compact jewellery boxes for ready-to-sell gift sets and premium add-on products.
Plain and branded gift bags with foil or digital print
Plain stock bags are useful when speed matters. They are a solid option for standard retail use, new product launches, temporary promotions or stores that need flexible stock across different product lines. Branded gift bags are the better fit when you want stronger shelf-to-checkout consistency and packaging that carries your logo clearly.
For printing, foil print gives a crisp, defined finish that works especially well for simple logos, metallic effects and luxury presentation. Digital print is better when you need more colour detail, tonal artwork or a broader design area. If you are not sure which method suits your artwork, Westpack can guide you through the branding workflow, from product selection and quote to artwork approval and lead times for printed production.

FAQs about gift bags, printing and ordering
Below are answers to common trade questions about sizing, printing, bulk ordering and choosing the right gift bags for retail packaging.
What size gift bag do I need for jewellery boxes?
Measure the product or box using width, depth and height, then choose a bag with enough room for easy packing and any added tissue paper or ribbon. Ring and earring boxes usually suit small bags, while necklace boxes, bracelet boxes and watch boxes often need more width or depth. For multi-item purchases, move up to a wider or deeper format so the contents sit neatly.
Can I order branded gift bags with my logo?
Yes. Westpack offers branded gift bags with logo printing for business use. You can choose a suitable bag style and discuss artwork, print method, order quantities and lead times before approving production. If needed, you can also order plain stock bags for immediate use while planning a printed range.
What is the difference between foil print and digital print on gift bags?
Foil print is often the better choice for simple logos, clean lines and a more defined finish, particularly on luxury gift bags. Digital print is more suitable for artwork with multiple colours, finer detail or larger printed areas. The best option depends on your design, the bag material and the quantity you need.
Are gift bags available in bulk or wholesale quantities?
Yes. Westpack supplies wholesale gift bags and gift bags in bulk for retailers, boutiques, jewellery businesses and gift shops. You can buy stock lines online for ongoing trade supply and speak to the team if you need support with larger repeat orders, seasonal planning or printed quantities.
Can I order samples before placing a larger order?
In many cases, yes. Sample ordering is useful if you want to check size, format, handle type, colour or product fit before committing to a larger purchase. It is a practical way to confirm suitability for jewellery boxes, accessories or gift sets and to build a coordinated packaging range with matching boxes and finishing accessories.
